Free tier specs

The official FAQ states the Free tier supports up to 3 agents, 2GB of storage, and community support; exceeding the user or storage caps upgrades the account automatically to a 14-day Standard trial. The official page states Jira Service Management is now part of the Service Collection, bundled with Customer Service Management, Assets, and Rovo.

Customers are free and unlimited

The official FAQ states customers (people raising requests) are free, need no Service Collection license, and are unlimited in number (“Customers are free and don't require a Service Collection license”). Agents and customers use different metering: agents cap at 3, customers are unlimited.

ITSM focus

The detail page notes an ITSM and service management focus (incidents, problems, changes, asset management); fit with a lightweight support setup is for the reader to judge. The virtual service agent is included only in the Premium and Enterprise tiers.

Paid tier

Standard $21.50/agent/month (price shown on the page, billing period not stated in the text read); 14-day trial, extendable to 30 days. Trials need no payment information (official FAQ: “Cancel any time, no payment information required”).

Overage and paid tiers

Overage: Exceeding the user or storage caps upgrades the account automatically to a 14-day Standard trial (official FAQ). Paid tiers: Standard $21.50/agent/month (price shown; billing period — check the official page).
