Chatwoot Community Edition explained

Published 2026-11-15 · Updated 2026-11-15 · Source: Chatwoot official self-hosted pricing page · Verified 2026-11-14

Self-hostedCommunity EditionLinuxPostgreSQLRedis

Chatwoot Community Edition deploys on owned infrastructure, with later upgrades possible and data and settings carried over on migration. Requirements are a Linux server, PostgreSQL, and Redis, with at least 4GB of memory and 2 CPU cores recommended for production, and S3 or S3-compatible storage for file uploads. Requirements

Official self-hosted page FAQ: a Linux server, PostgreSQL, and Redis; file uploads need S3 or S3-compatible storage; deployment guides cover Docker, Kubernetes, and major cloud providers; production use calls for at least 4GB of memory and 2 CPU cores.

Feature boundaries and support channels

Captain AI comes with the Premium Support and Enterprise tiers, connecting to OpenAI GPT models; Community Edition support channels are Discord and GitHub; Premium Support adds priority email support, and Enterprise adds phone support and developer office hours. Custom branding appears in the Premium Support tier difference notes; Community Edition branding coverage is not stated — check the official page.

Relation to the Cloud free tier

The Community Edition and the Cloud free tier are two separate entries, with entirely different billing and operating costs. The Community Edition runs on owned infrastructure, with server costs and maintenance time counted separately; the Cloud free tier follows cloud tier rules. See the self-hosted vs hosted comparison page.
