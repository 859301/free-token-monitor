Time-limited free plan

The official help page states the Free Program serves small businesses and startups, with 6 months free; the program applies to Freshdesk only, not to Freshdesk Omni; upgrading to a paid tier is required at expiry or with more agents. This page marks the plan as a time-limited free tier, kept separate from permanent free tiers.

How to join

Inside a Freshdesk account, go to Admin > Account > Plans and Billing > Switch plan > Try out our Free Program, review the changes and click Yes, Proceed.

Overage and paid tiers

Overage: Past 6 months or past 2 agents, upgrading to a paid tier is required; once upgraded, you cannot switch back to the Free Program. Paid tiers: Paid tier prices — check the official page.

FAQ

What happens after the 6 months?

Upgrading to a paid tier is required at expiry; more than 2 agents also requires an upgrade to a paid tier.

Does the Free Program apply to Freshdesk Omni?

No. The program applies to Freshdesk only, not to Freshdesk Omni.

How is the Free Program joined?

Inside a Freshdesk account, go to Admin > Account > Plans and Billing, click Switch plan, click Try out our Free Program, review the changes and click Yes, Proceed.

Can Free Program users try higher tiers?

Yes. The official help page says existing and new Free Program users can try higher plans for 14 days; that is a trial of the higher plans, not of the Free Program itself. Once you upgrade to a paid tier, you cannot switch back to the Free Program.
