Free tier specs

The official page states “The Free plan includes 5 users, 1 Inbox, and 1 Docs site”. The comparison table Free column reads: users “Up to 5”, contacts “100/mo”, 1 inbox, 1 Docs site, 10 articles, 10 saved replies, 10 tags. The FAQ also confirms the Free tier limit of 1 inbox and 1 Docs site. Contacts are unlimited on paid tiers.

How contacts are counted

Official definition: a contact is a person who received a reply from the team or had a question resolved by AI; multiple conversations with the same person count as one contact. The 100 contacts a month cap counts people, not conversation threads.

30 days is report history

Pre-built reports carry 30 days of history; that figure is report history, not conversation retention. The official page does not state conversation record retention, and the two figures are kept apart — check the official page.

Channels and paid tiers

Live chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger appear in the Standard tier feature cards; the Free tier description carries no live chat, and whether the Free tier includes live chat — check the official page. Email is the core inbox capability. Paid tiers: Standard $25/user/month; Plus $45/user/month; Pro $75/user/month (annual billing discount noted on the page); Envoy AI add-on $0.75 per resolution, with a 3-month free Envoy trial for new accounts. Extra inboxes $10/month (annual) or $12/month; extra Docs sites $20/month (annual) or $24/month.

Overage and paid tiers

Overage: Check the official page. Paid tiers: Standard $25/user/month; Plus $45/user/month; Pro $75/user/month; Envoy AI $0.75 per resolution.

FAQ

Is 100 contacts a month enough?

That depends on the number of distinct people contacted each month: multiple conversations with the same person count as one contact. Contacts are unlimited on paid tiers.

How many help articles does the Free tier allow?

1 Docs site and 10 articles. Extra Docs sites cost $20/month (annual) or $24/month.

Does a free account need a payment method?

No. The official FAQ states free accounts need no payment method.
