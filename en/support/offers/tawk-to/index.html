What the free core covers

The official llms.txt describes Tawk.to as a free omnichannel inbox and communication platform, with free core capabilities covering the omnichannel inbox, ticketing platform, knowledge base, Pipeline and CRM, form builder, and calendar integrations. Seats are unlimited, with no per-seat fees. Monthly conversation, contact, and ticket caps are not stated in the official text read — check the official page; “unlimited conversations” is not claimed.

Add-on prices

Add-on prices on the official help page: Remove Branding $39/month, or $348/year (equivalent to $29/month), covering branding replacement on the widget, ticket emails, and knowledge base, plus a custom domain; Video + Voice + Screensharing $49/month, or $348/year (equivalent to $29/month); Hired Agents from $1/hour, varying by hours and coverage type. AI Assist has 4 tiers, with prices shown as images and not read in the text — check the official page.

Conflicting wording on official pages

The llms.txt product section states “Every feature, every channel, every tool — no plan gates, no add-on costs”; the help page from the same vendor lists several paid add-ons with prices. Both wordings appear side by side, with add-on charges stated from the help page prices.

Channels

Channels in the official llms.txt include the tawk.to widget, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, SMS, email, and Instagram. Whether all are part of the free core is not distinguished item by item in the official text read — check the official page.
