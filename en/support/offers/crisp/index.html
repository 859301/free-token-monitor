Free tier specs

The Free tier costs $0/month, with 2 seats and a 100 customer profile cap. The Free tier feature list carries the website chat widget and the contact form. On conversations, the official page describes per-workspace pricing, “regardless of the number of conversations”, and the comparison table carries an “Unlimited conversations” row; further contact and conversation metering is not stated — check the official page. AI credits on the Free tier are $0.

Trial and Free tier are separate

The official page states that every new account can try any tier free for 14 days (a full-feature experience). Features visible during the trial do not equal Free tier coverage: after the trial, coverage follows tier rules, with tier placement below.

Where features sit

The shared email inbox appears in the Mini tier additions; the omnichannel inbox (WhatsApp Business, Instagram DM, SMS, Line, Viber) appears in the Essentials tier; the knowledge base appears in the Essentials tier features; the ticketing system and customer portal appear in the Plus tier features; white labelling, removing the Crisp watermark, is a Plus tier feature. Whether the Free tier covers email, Telegram, Messenger, or other channels could not be read from comparison table check marks — check the official page.

Paid tiers

Mini $45/month (4 seats, $5 AI credits, about 90 automated conversations); Essentials $95/month (10 seats, $25 AI credits, about 450 automated conversations); Plus $295/month (20+ seats, $75 AI credits, about 1350 automated conversations). The comparison table marks extra seats at $10/month in the Plus column only.

Overage and paid tiers

Overage: Check the official page. Paid tiers: Mini $45/month; Essentials $95/month; Plus $295/month; extra seats $10/month in the Plus column.

FAQ

Are conversations really unlimited on the Crisp Free tier?

The official page describes per-workspace pricing, and the comparison table carries an “Unlimited conversations” row; further metering is not stated — check the official page. The 100 customer profile cap is a separate limit.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

The trial is a full-feature experience, separate from the Free tier. Post-trial tier handling — check the official page.

Can the Free tier remove the Crisp watermark?

No. White labelling, removing the watermark, is a Plus tier feature, at $295/month.
