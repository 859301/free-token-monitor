What the free tools cover

The free tools cover the shared inbox (team email), HubSpot Smart CRM contact management, live chat and conversational bots (limited features), a centralized ticketing system, and basic reports and dashboards. The ticketing system is part of the free tools; the knowledge base and customer portal sit in the Professional tier, absent from the free tools.

Branding

The free tools carry HubSpot branding; the Starter tier adds “Removal of HubSpot branding”, so branding removal needs a paid tier.

Paid tiers

Paid tiers (official blog summary, annual billing): Starter from $7/seat/month; Professional from $90/seat/month, plus a $1,500 one-time onboarding fee; Enterprise from $150/seat/month, plus a $3,500 one-time onboarding fee. HubSpot Credits: 500 a month on Starter, 3,000 on Professional, 5,000 on Enterprise.

Overage and paid tiers

Overage: Check the official page. Paid tiers: Starter from $7/seat/month; Professional from $90/seat/month + $1,500 onboarding fee; Enterprise from $150/seat/month + $3,500 onboarding fee (annual billing).
