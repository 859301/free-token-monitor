Free tier and trial rules

The official page states “Zoho Desk's free plan offers three user licenses”. A 15-day free trial downgrades automatically to the Free Edition, which “has no billing”. The trial needs no credit card (official pricing FAQ: “without ever providing a credit card”).

Email ticketing as the core

The Free Edition is described as easy email ticketing. Instant messaging (including WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, WeChat, and Line) appears in the Standard tier feature list, as does the knowledge base. Whether the Free Edition includes a knowledge base is not stated in the text read — check the official page.

What the Free Edition excludes

The official pricing FAQ states the Free Edition excludes light users, automation, multi-department support, SLA, AI features, and custom roles and profiles, available after an upgrade.
