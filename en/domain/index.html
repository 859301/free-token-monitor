Services not in the list, and why

The following services do not appear in the tables above; the reasons are stated below as facts.

  • Freenom: free domains are dead: new registrations stopped in 2023, exit from the domain business in 2024, paid sales of .tk / .cf / .gq resumed 2026-07-23.
  • eu.org: free subdomains appear only in second-hand claims (manual review, several days to several weeks/months, real name and address required, small commercial sites discouraged); current terms on the vendor official site are unconfirmed.
  • JS.ORG: free subdomains appear only in second-hand claims mentioning their existence; current terms on the official site are unconfirmed.
  • DigitalPlat FreeDomain: appears only in second-hand claims (dpdns.org, qzz.io, us.kg, xx.kg, qd.je, default 1 year, renewable free, no SLA); current terms on the official site are unconfirmed; one source warns the old Telegram channel was hijacked and to trust only GitHub/the console.
  • is-a-good.dev: appears only in second-hand claims mentioning its existence; current terms on the official site are unconfirmed.
  • Dynadot: appears only in forum posts mentioning Black Friday promos; current prices on the official site are unconfirmed.
  • Google Domains: sold to Squarespace; no longer operating as a standalone registrar.

Change log (per official pages and docs dates)

  • Early 2023: Freenom stopped new registrations (second-hand reports).
