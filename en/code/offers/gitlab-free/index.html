FAQ

Does GitLab Free sign-up need a credit card?

No. The pricing page states “No credit card required”. Using shared runners still requires card verification. Verified 2026-11-13.

Is the $1 GitLab shared-runner check charged?

No. The $1 amount is an authorization check, with no charge. A bring-your-own runner, or disabling shared runners, needs no card. Verified 2026-11-13.

What does GitLab Ultimate cost?

No public price. The pricing page states custom pricing (quote-based pricing). Premium is $29/user/month, annual billing. Verified 2026-11-13.
