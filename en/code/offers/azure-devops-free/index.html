Users and licenses

The pricing page states that the first 5 users are free, then $6 per user per month. The FAQ states that unlimited users can be added to an organization with free access to Pipelines and Artifacts; when other services such as Repos or Boards are enabled, the first five users get a Basic license for free, subsequent users default to the Stakeholder tier, and Basic licenses are needed for access to Repos and Boards. Visual Studio subscribers get free access to Azure DevOps and do not count against the 5 free users.

Pipeline minutes

Each organization gets one parallel job with 1,800 minutes (30 hours) of build time every month using Microsoft-hosted agents, plus one free self-hosted agent running one parallel job with unlimited minutes. Free build amounts reset on the first day of the month. Buying a first Microsoft-hosted pipeline keeps the parallel job count at one and only removes the time limit on the free pipeline; two concurrent jobs need two Microsoft-hosted pipelines. Extra jobs cost $40 per Microsoft-hosted job and $15 per self-hosted job per month.

Artifacts

The first 2 GiB per organization are free, with tiered pricing beyond that: $2 per GiB for 2–10 GiB, $1 per GiB for 10–100 GiB, $0.5 per GiB for 100–1,000 GiB, and $0.25 per GiB above 1,000 GiB.

Paid plans

Basic costs $6/user/month; Basic + Test Plans costs $52/user/month. The pricing page states that prices are estimates only and not actual price quotes, and that actual pricing may vary by agreement type, purchase date, and currency exchange rate.

Unconfirmed information

Whether the free allowances are permanent, and repository storage and transfer allowances, are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.

FAQ

What do the first 5 free users get on Azure DevOps?

The first 5 Basic users are free, including unlimited private Git repositories, Boards, 1,800 Microsoft-hosted pipeline minutes per month, 1 self-hosted job with unlimited minutes, and 2 GiB of Artifacts per organization. Verified 2026-11-14.

What happens when the 1,800 minutes run out?

Free build amounts reset on the first day of the month; extra parallel jobs can be bought at $40 per Microsoft-hosted job and $15 per self-hosted job per month. Buying a first Microsoft-hosted job keeps the parallel job count at one and only removes the time limit on the free pipeline. Verified 2026-11-14.

Does getting started with Azure DevOps need a credit card?

No; the pricing page FAQ states a credit card is not necessary to get started. A card is set up in the purchase experience when more services or more users are added. Verified 2026-11-14.
