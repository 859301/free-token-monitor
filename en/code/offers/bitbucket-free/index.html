Bitbucket Free

Bitbucket · Verified 2026-11-14 · Page updated 2026-11-14

Free tier Up to 5 users 50 build minutes/month Build minutes scale by runner size

Bitbucket Free $0: up to 5 users, unlimited public/private repositories, 50 build minutes per month, 1 GB LFS file storage (plus 1 GB storage per repository). Build minutes and storage are shared by all users in the workspace; build minutes scale by runner size, so a 5-minute step on a 2x runner counts as 10 minutes. How build minutes are counted

The pricing page FAQ states that build minutes measure CI/CD pipeline execution time on Atlassian cloud runners, scaled by runner size: a 5-minute step on a 2x runner counts as 10 build minutes. The documentation limitations page states the monthly build minutes per plan: 50 minutes per month on the Free plan. Build minutes and storage are shared by all users in the workspace.

Storage wording

The pricing page FAQ states 1 GB of file storage (currently through LFS), in addition to 1 GB of storage per repository. The documentation limitations page states 1 GB of large file storage in total on the Free plan. Both wordings are presented side by side — check the official page.

Paid plans and the price difference

The pricing page states Standard $3.65/user/month and Premium $8/user/month. The documentation limitations page states Standard $3/user/month (flat rate of $15/month for 1–5 users) and Premium $6/user/month (flat rate of $30/month for 1–5 users). The two price wordings differ; both are presented side by side with no ruling on which is newer — check the official page. The documentation page states 2,500 build minutes per month on Standard and 3,500 on Premium.

Community and academic subscriptions

The pricing page FAQ states that community or academic subscriptions include unlimited private repositories for unlimited contributors, 5 GB of LFS file storage, and 500 build minutes, subject to eligibility (automatic conversion with an academic email address, or an application for a community subscription). Eligibility-based subscriptions are stated separately from the free tier.

Unconfirmed information

Free-tier card requirements, whether the free tier is permanent, and whether pipelines stop when build minutes run out are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. The documentation page only states that the Free plan has no overage protection (Overage protection: Not included).
