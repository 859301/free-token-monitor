Free allowances come in four shapes: long-term free tiers (no end date, rate-limited), signup credits (one-time, with an expiry), periodic resets (daily, weekly, or monthly), and limited-time campaigns (a fixed end date). Mixing them up is the most common source of confusion — see the free-tier types comparison.
