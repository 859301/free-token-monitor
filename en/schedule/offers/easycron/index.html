How EPD is counted

EPD (Executions Per Day) counts total executions of a cron job on its busiest day. Per the official FAQ: a job running every 10 minutes occupies 144 EPDs, every minute 1,440 EPDs; deleting a job releases occupied EPDs back to the balance.

Monthly expiry and excluded features

The Free tier expires monthly (Expires Monthly); renewal terms and the state of jobs after expiry are not stated on the pages read — check the official page.

The Free tier excludes email notification, failure log retention, custom HTTP, Slack notification, regexp matching, webhook notification, and subusers. The timeout is 5 seconds, and the minimum interval is 20 minutes.

FAQ

How many jobs do 200 EPDs cover?

Frequency decides: 2 jobs every 20 minutes, 4 every 30 minutes, 8 every hour, 200 every day; no allowance for every-minute or every-10-minute jobs.

What does monthly expiry mean for the Free tier?

The pricing page states Expires Monthly, meaning the Free tier expires monthly; renewal terms and the state of jobs after expiry are not stated on the pages read — check the official page.

Is the timeout really only 5 seconds?

Yes. The Free tier timeout is 5 seconds, and a request taking longer is treated as a timeout.
