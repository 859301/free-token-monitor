Free scope and terms boundaries

Terms section 1: the service is entirely free of charge, no fees have to be paid. Section 4: no warranty of any form, including uptime and performance guarantees, and no liability. Section 6: the service may be shut down at any time without prior notice; accounts may be deleted or locked without prior notice and without reasons. Section 7: the service is not intended for processing personal data, sensitive company data, or other confidential data; URLs, headers, and payloads must not carry secrets, passwords, or personal data.

Job count caps, execution caps, the default timeout value, response data caps, history retention counts, and retry rules are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.

Schedules and API

Schedule fields are minutes, hours, days of month, months, and days of week; minutes [-1] means every minute. Each job takes a timezone, UTC by default; timeouts are per job in seconds, with -1 meaning the default timeout, whose value is unstated.

The REST API allows 100 requests a day by default and 5,000 a day for sustaining members; job creation is limited to 1 request per second and 5 per minute; listing, details, and history are limited to 5 requests per second; over-limit requests return 429.

Sustaining memberships

Sustaining memberships are voluntary monthly or yearly fees and do not result in additional entitlements (scope, features, availability); extra features or relaxed limits are provided only on a voluntary basis at discretion. Memberships can be cancelled at any time, effective at the end of the period, with benefits revoked.

Overage and paid tiers

API requests past the daily limit return 429. Job count caps, execution caps, the default timeout, and response data caps are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.
