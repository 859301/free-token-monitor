Services outside the tables, and why

  • Self-hosted queue software and workflow engines: outside the scope of this section, which covers standalone hosted services only.

Comparison note on platform built-ins

When built-in scheduling on the main stack covers the need, built-in scheduling keeps the setup simpler; standalone services add independent retries, delays, dead-letter queues, and cross-platform triggers. Metering units differ across services (messages, steps, executions, compute seconds, EPD); see Standalone services vs platform built-ins.
