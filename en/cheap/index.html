The Cheap section’s three rules

A plan enters the Cheap section only if it meets all three; failing any one sends it to the Deals section:

  • Standing supply: on a standing catalog or specials page, with no stated end date.
  • Standing price: same-rate renewal stated, or the price is itself a list price (e.g., monthly-capped cloud pricing with no first-term discount).
  • Buyable any time: orderable outside Black Friday, Singles’ Day, anniversary, and other promo windows.

Services not in the list, and why

The following vendors or claims did not meet the Cheap section’s three rules when checked, so they are not in the list. The reasons are given below — not as recommendations.

  • BandwagonHost entry price: third parties contradict each other on $49.99/year vs $99.99/year, and report plan removals around October 30, 2026; official-page verification was not completed.
  • IONOS: the official page only gives a from-$2/month headline price (the page title says from $4/month); plan specs and renewal prices are not listed on the page and could not be verified; a 30-day refund guarantee is not free. (Checked 2026-11-13.)
  • OVHcloud VPS 2027: the official page lists VPS-1 from $4.54/month (2 vCore / 4 GB / 40 GB NVMe) but does not say whether the from-price requires a prepayment commitment, and the renewal price is also unstated; third-party reviews say the from-price is a 12-month prepay price (unverified). The three standing-price rules are not met. (Checked 2026-11-13.)
