Renewal promise

Official Specials page FAQ, quoted verbatim: “Can I renew a RackNerd VPS special at the same rate? Yes. These RackNerd VPS specials are lifetime recurring. You can renew at the same rate for as long as you decide to keep the service.” This is the only vendor in the Cheap section whose page explicitly promises lifetime same-rate renewal — and the reason this site ranks it first.

What’s included

The ctrl panel includes reboot, power on/off, OS reinstall, HTML5 VNC console, one-click apps, network firewall, root password reset, and upgrades. One-click apps include AdminBolt, OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, AnythingLLM, Ollama, LibreChat, n8n, Coolify, Docker/Portainer, WordPress panels, and more. Memory thresholds from the official FAQ: OpenClaw needs at least 8 GB; Hermes, AnythingLLM, Ollama, and LibreChat need at least 4 GB; n8n and Coolify start at 2 GB. Match the memory threshold before choosing a tier.

Locations & setup

The page says multi-location deployment (deploy in multiple locations) and lets you pick a data center near your users, but lists no specific cities on the Specials page; the FAQ likewise only says multiple RackNerd locations. Go by the locations shown at order time. Setup is instant: deployed right after payment, online within minutes.
