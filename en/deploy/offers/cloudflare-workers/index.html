FAQ

What happens when the 100,000 daily requests run out?

What happens after exceeding the free tier is not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. The paid tier starts at $5/month/account, including 10M requests/month, then +$0.30/M. Verified 2026-11-13.

What are the KV and D1 free amounts?

KV Free: 100,000 reads/day, 1,000 writes/day, 1 GB storage. D1 Free: 5M row reads/day, 100,000 row writes/day, 5 GB total storage, 10 databases. Verified 2026-11-13.

What is the difference between Workers and Pages?

The metering differs: Workers is metered by requests and CPU (100,000 requests/day, 10 ms CPU per invocation); Pages is limited by builds and files (500 builds/month, 20,000 files/site, single file 25 MiB). Verified 2026-11-13.
