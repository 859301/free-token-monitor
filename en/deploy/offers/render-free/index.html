The three free service types

The pricing page FAQ confirms: “With Render’s Free compute plans, you can spin up web services, Render Key Value instances, and Render Postgres databases at no charge.” Free compute plans have usage limits, for exploring new technologies, personal projects, and previewing the Render developer experience.

Sleep rules

Free instances spin down after 15 minutes without inbound traffic; wake-up takes about 1 minute. Free instance hours are 750 hours/workspace/month, pausing when used up, resetting monthly, with no rollover.

Free Postgres: expires after 30 days

1 free Postgres per workspace, 1 GB, expiring 30 days after creation; after a 14-day grace period, deleted along with the data. Free Key Value: 1 per workspace, in-memory only.

Production use and upgrades

The official page states free instances should not be used for production (“Do not use them for production”). Upgrading the workspace plan does not lift the free instance limits. Without a payment method, exceeding bandwidth pauses the service, with no charge.
