Free VPS: verified free cloud servers

Updated 2026-11-14 · 10 verified entries (8 verified 2026-11-12, InstaBox and EUserv verified 2026-11-13) · Specs and terms transcribed from vendor official pages

The bottom line: only 2 VMs are truly free forever — Oracle Always Free and Google Cloud e2-micro — and both require a card. The other 5 are time-limited trials or credits (AWS, Azure, Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, Kamatera), 1 is students-only (Azure for Students, the only one explicitly card-free), and 1 is a free sandbox (InstaBox, a 4 vCPU / 8 GiB machine, not a traditional VPS), and 1 is free but needs manual renewal every month (EUserv VS2-free, IPv6-only). The five groups stay separate: trials are never written as permanent. Every entry states its card, region, reclaim, and expiry rules, with the verification date and official link in the table.

This section follows the same rule as the free credits overview: only content checked against vendor official pages — no invite links, resellers, or task-renewal hosts. Pick a group first, then open the detail page for the entry you want.

Services not in the list, and why

The following vendors or claims could not be verified on an official page, are not sold by the vendor itself, or have unclear terms, so they are not shown as verified offers. The reasons are given below — not as recommendations.

  • Huawei Cloud free trial: the official free page returned homepage content when checked; no current spec card was verified, status unclear.
  • Vultr and DigitalOcean new-user credits: second-hand sources contradict each other on amounts and validity, no official page was verified, and most are tied to referral links (see the Deals section, where they are under deal rules).
  • Cloudways: no official page verified, and it is a managed layer (reselling other clouds’ resources), which fails the vendor-direct-only rule.
  • AccuWebHosting free Windows VPS: second-hand sources only, no official page verified.
  • Baidu AI Cloud BCC free trial: only a 2021 article was found, no current official page — treated as ended or status unclear.
  • GratisVPS, VPSWala, and other small “permanently free” hosts: no verifiable official terms or business information.
  • Abeiyun, Sanfengyun, Rainyun, and other task-renewal hosts: require posting, check-ins, or points to renew, which fails the official-terms-only rule. (Sanfengyun is in the Deals section under deal rules.)
  • IONOS, Hostinger, and other “money-back guarantee” offers: a refund guarantee is not a free trial.
  • Ended claims: Oracle Always Free 4 OCPU / 24 GB (docs changed to 2 OCPU / 12 GB in June 2026); AWS 12-month free EC2 (new accounts moved to the credit model). Tutorials still writing these are out of date.

Added 2026-11-13: trial credits (Vultr, DigitalOcean, Akamai, Hetzner) and Sanfengyun are in the VPS Deals section under deal rules (verified on official pages, expiry dates and risks stated, marked separately from free).
