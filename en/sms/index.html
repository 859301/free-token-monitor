SMS Verification

10 entries · Vendor-verified · Updated 2026-11-16

SMS verification sits under the authentication stage. Most services are time-limited trials or pay-as-you-go; no service offers a permanent free tier for unlimited recipients. The closest ongoing allowances repeat daily in small amounts: GCP Identity Platform bills nothing for the first 10 SMS per day, and the Textbelt public key sends 1 free text per day. Billing runs in three layers: verification platform fees, channel fees, and carrier surcharges, counted separately. Three billing layers

Verification platform fee: Verify services charge per successful verification (Twilio $0.05 each, Vonage $0.06084 each), or charge zero verification fees with channel charges only (Plivo, Bird with no platform fee). Channel fee: billed per message or per segment, plus carrier surcharges; long messages bill per split segment. Carrier surcharges: charged separately by carrier and number type, with amounts on each detail page. Whether failed attempts are billed differs by service: Twilio skips the base fee for failed or expired attempts but always bills SMS attempts. See the Verify billing comparison and the FAQ.

AWS SNS clarification note

The AWS SNS Free Tier does not include SMS, and the old 100 free messages claim has no official basis. See the AWS SNS clarification note.

Why some services have no price or detail page

Huawei Cloud SMS stopped sending services on 2025-04-25. Ihuyi prices and test message counts — check the official page. Number-rental services are virtual numbers for receiving codes, a different category from SMS APIs for sending codes.
