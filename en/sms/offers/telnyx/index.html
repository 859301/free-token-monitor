SMS prices

The messaging pricing page RATES area offers sender-type selection, with three SMS groups: Local incl. 10DLC, Toll-Free, and Short Code (a further RCS tab covers RCS messages, not SMS). United States (Country: United States, Currency: USD): Local incl. 10DLC sending $0.004 per part plus carrier fee, receiving $0.004 per part; Toll-Free sending $0.0055 per part plus carrier fee, receiving $0.0055 per part; Short Code sending $0.007 per part plus carrier fee, receiving $0.007 per part. China (switching Country to China leaves Alphanumeric Sender ID as the only sender type): sending $0.038 per part, with MMS and receiving both N/A.

MMS and volume tiers

MMS: sending $0.015 per part, receiving $0.005 per part, both plus carrier fees. Volumes above 100 million parts per month drop to automatic tiered pricing. Carrier fee example: Verizon outbound $0.0045 per part.

Conflicting wording, shown side by side

  • Verify pricing: the page header says From $0.05 a verification, with Delivery cost is inside the rate; no separate messaging line for verify. The rate table Services row says Use Verify API via SMS $0.03 per successful verification + SMS API pricing, with voice, flash call, and WhatsApp rows following the same $0.03 plus API pricing structure. The two wordings differ and are shown side by side, with no ruling.
  • Trial allowance: developer docs (updated 2026-10-16) state USD $5 in testing credit is provided, limited to 1 verified number, 10 lifetime changes, 15 lifetime delivery attempts, long-code messages only to verified numbers at most 100 per day, Verify SMS-only at most 50 per day, trial numbers reclaimed if not upgraded within 30 days, and credit card as the only payment method. A support article (updated 2026-10-14) states Telnyx may provide free trial credit from time to time... however at this moment in time there are no promotions that provide free trial credit. The developer docs are 2 days newer, stated as a fact, with no ruling. The support article is also internally inconsistent: the same article mentions a SIP Trunking use case purchased using their free sign-up credit.

Overage and paid tiers

Pay-as-you-go pricing, with automatic tiered pricing above 100 million parts per month. Further overage details are on the official page.

FAQ

What does Verify actually cost per verification?

The header says $0.05 per verification with delivery cost inside the rate, while the table says $0.03 per successful verification plus SMS API pricing; the two wordings differ and are shown side by side — check the official page.

Is trial credit available?

Developer docs state a $5 testing credit, while a support article states no promotion currently provides free trial credit; the two directly conflict — check the official page.

Is a card required?

The pay-as-you-go $0 tier and the Verify pricing page both state Card on file; developer docs limit trial payment methods to credit card.
