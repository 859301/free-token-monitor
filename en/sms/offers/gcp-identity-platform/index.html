First 10 SMS per day not billed

From the pricing page: The first ten SMS that you send per day are not billed. The allowance repeats daily and is the closest to a long-term free SMS allowance among the services verified.

Per-message prices and example

Beyond the daily allowance: US $0.01, China $0.01, Canada $0.01, India $0.07, UK $0.04. The pricing table lists China (CN) at US$0.01 / 1 count and the US at the same figure. Pricing page example: sending 1,300 SMS, with 300 free at $0 and 1,000 at 0.01 for $10.

MAU free tier counted separately

Identity Platform monthly active users up to 50,000 are free; that free tier covers monthly active user counts and is counted separately from SMS volume, never to be conflated. The Firebase pricing page states Phone Auth is billed per SMS sent, with 50K MAU at no cost.

Card requirements

Card requirements are unverified. The Firebase FAQ page carries no Phone Auth or SMS card wording; Authentication appears only in a sentence pointing to another FAQ page; the Pricing section mentions only the Blaze plan and budget alerts, with no card mention. The Identity Platform pricing page states no card or credit card requirement, with billing appearing only in a sentence about opening the billing page in the API console. The claim of a Cloud Billing association requirement since September 2024 was not confirmed in official text read and is not stated as fact — check the official page.

Overage and paid tiers

Messages beyond the first 10 per day are billed per message. Further overage details are on the official page.

Unconfirmed information

  • Card requirements: neither the FAQ page nor the pricing page states a requirement; the September 2024 claim is unconfirmed in official text.
  • Mainland compliance details: unverified.
