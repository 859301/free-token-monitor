Alibaba Cloud SMS explained

Published 2026-11-16 · Updated 2026-11-16 · Source: Alibaba Cloud international SMS pricing page · Verified 2026-11-15

Trial pack 100/200 messages3 monthsIndividual qualification limitsUSD and CNY prices kept separate

Alibaba Cloud SMS offers a free trial pack: 100 domestic messages for individually verified accounts and 200 for enterprise-verified accounts, valid 3 months, for verification and notification only, with one participation per user. International USD prices and mainland CNY prices belong to two separate sites and are written separately: the international China package starts at $150 for 10,000 messages with pay-as-you-go China pricing at $0.0500 per message, while mainland packages start at CNY 50 for 1,000 messages. Self-use qualifications for individual users will not pass real-name signature registration; a third-party qualification or an upgrade to an enterprise account is required. Verified 2026-11-15.

Free trial pack

SMS offers two ways to test for free: an SMS Skill quick test, sending 1 free test message after installing the Skill, which cannot replace qualification, signature, or template review; and a free trial pack, with 100 domestic messages for individual verification and 200 for enterprise verification, valid 3 months, for verification and notification only. Conditions are completed identity verification, a new SMS user, and no arrears; each user can participate once; overage or expiry is charged at standard prices; domestic SMS is 70 characters per message, splitting at 67 characters when longer. A 2024 promotion post offering 100 free messages for new enterprise users (excluding mainland China) has expired and is not treated as a current allowance.

International USD prices

China SMS Package: 10,000 messages $150 ($0.015 per message), 50,000 $650 ($0.013), 100,000 $1,200 ($0.012), 200,000 $2,000 ($0.01), all valid 180 days; Global Plus-100 $10, Global-300 $15, valid 180 days. Page footer: Alibaba Cloud SMS becomes unavailable when your SMS package runs out. Pay-as-you-go table (USD per message): China 0.0500, US 0.0086, Hong Kong 0.0577, Macao 0.0500, India 0.0816, Singapore 0.0368, Thailand 0.0060. The default billing mode is resource packages; pay-as-you-go requires contacting sales; SMS charges on submission regardless of delivery outcome; metering is real-time with settlement on the 1st of the next month. The same destination, China, works out to $0.010–$0.015 per message under packages and is priced at $0.0500 per message under pay-as-you-go; the two billing modes are written separately. Sender ID fees: US long code registration 9 plus monthly fee 9, short code registration 2000 plus monthly fee 1000 (amounts carry no currency label in the table; page context is USD).

Mainland CNY prices

Packages (CNY): 1,000 messages CNY 50/0.05, 2,000 CNY 100/0.05, 5,000 CNY 250/0.05, 15,000 CNY 705/0.047, 50,000 CNY 2,250/0.045, 200,000 CNY 8,800/0.044, 500,000 CNY 21,500/0.043, 1,000,000 CNY 42,000/0.042, 3,000,000 CNY 123,000/0.041, valid 2 years. Monthly tiered pay-as-you-go: verification and notification 0.045/0.042/0.041/0.040/0.039/0.038 CNY, promotion 0.055/0.052/0.051/0.050/0.049/0.048 CNY; individually verified users cannot apply for promotion templates; domestic SMS must be sent from a mainland China IP address. China-site international pay-as-you-go table (unit price CNY): China 0.3896, US 0.0672, Hong Kong 0.4497, India 0.6357; overseas SMS is charged on submission. A help-site summary mislabels the US 0.0672 figure as USD; the table header CNY governs.

Signature and qualification review

From the help docs: Self-use qualifications for individual users will not pass the real-name signature registration. You must request a third-party qualification or upgrade your Alibaba Cloud account to an enterprise account. Alibaba Cloud qualification review usually completes within 2 working days; carrier real-name registration averages 5–7 working days, 7–10 for some carriers, with no timing guarantee from carriers and actual times possibly longer; domestic SMS signature authorization is limited to mainland China companies; international/Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan SMS needs no qualification, signature, or template application; verification hours are 9:00–19:00, Monday to Sunday.

Billing and splitting rules

GSM-7 pure English: 160 characters per message, splitting at 153 characters when longer; UCS-2 non-English: 70 characters per message, splitting at 67 characters when longer. SMS charges on submission regardless of delivery outcome.

Conflicting wording, shown side by side

  • Global Plus package table unit-price column is arithmetically inconsistent: the unit-price column does not match price divided by quantity, for example 120 messages at $20 with a $0.2 unit-price column (which would imply $24); 30,000 messages at $3,000 with a $0.2 unit-price column (which would imply $6,000); 50,000 messages at $5,000 likewise. This page only cites per-message figures for the four China SMS Package tiers, does not cite the Global Plus large-tier unit-price column, and notes the inconsistency side by side.

Overage and paid tiers

Trial pack overage or expiry is charged at standard prices; SMS becomes unavailable when the package runs out. Further overage details are on the official page.

FAQ

Can individuals use Alibaba Cloud SMS?

Individually verified accounts can claim a 100-message trial pack, but self-use qualifications fail real-name signature registration; a third-party qualification or enterprise upgrade is required, and individually verified users cannot apply for promotion templates.

How is the trial pack claimed?

Conditions are completed identity verification, a new SMS user, and no arrears, with one participation per user; 100 messages for individuals and 200 for enterprises, valid 3 months, verification and notification only.

How do USD and CNY prices differ?

International USD prices and mainland CNY prices belong to two separate sites: international pay-as-you-go China pricing is $0.0500 per message, while mainland packages run CNY 0.041–0.05 per message; the two are written separately, never mixed.
