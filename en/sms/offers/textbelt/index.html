1 free text per day

From the homepage: Try it now: use key=textbelt to send 1 free text per day. Create your own key to send more messages afterwards. The homepage carries no prices.

Purchase tiers

With the SMS to location selector set to US/Canada, the Cost tiers show $3/50 texts (selected by default), then $5/200 texts, $10/700 texts, $23/1700 texts, and $45/3500 texts; before a location is selected, the Cost column reads Select your location to get prices. Charges are one-time, processed by Stripe, with a 30-day guarantee.

Overage and paid tiers

The public key sends 1 text per day; sending more requires a personal key and a purchased tier. Further overage details are on the official page.
