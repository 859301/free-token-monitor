First-activation gift

The first-activation gift package: 200 messages for enterprise-verified users and 100 for individually verified users, claimed with a CNY 0 order, effective in 5 minutes, valid 3 months from issuance, one per verified entity, first-activation accounts only, non-stackable. Since 2026-03-24, the enterprise first-activation gift has been adjusted to 200 messages. The 1,000-message figure in the older review-standards PDF is outdated. The gift is a one-time first-activation gift, not a permanent free tier.

Mainland prices

Fixed packages: 10,000 messages CNY 470/0.047, 100,000 CNY 4,500/0.045, 500,000 CNY 22,500/0.045, 1,000,000 CNY 43,000/0.043, 3,000,000 CNY 123,000/0.041 (same price for enterprises and individuals; individuals verification and notification only); custom packages start at 1,000 messages, tiered 0.050/0.047/0.045/0.043/0.041; packages are valid 2 years from purchase. Mainland SMS is prepaid, does not support coupons, and requires a package first (sending without a package fails with error code 1031); billing follows successful submission.

International prices

International SMS packages: fixed $100/$500/$1,000/$5,000/$10,000, custom packages at least $10; effective 5 minutes after purchase, until used up; Singapore bills postpaid daily for tax reasons (package balance must stay above $0 with sufficient account funds); batch sends with insufficient balance automatically switch the excess to daily postpaid billing (once enabled). International per-message prices (USD, excluding tax): US 0.012, UK 0.046, India 0.078, Hong Kong 0.06, Japan 0.06, Singapore 0.055 (Singapore on postpaid daily settlement). International fixed packages also come in CNY tiers of 500/1,000/5,000/10,000/50,000 CNY, custom at least CNY 50; the international CNY price table lists the US at 0.0924 CNY per message. The cutoff date for default pay-as-you-go international billing differs between sites: the China-site docs say before 2025-05-27, the international-site docs say before August 25, 2025; each is written per the cited site.

Signature and template review

An SMS consists of a signature plus body text; signatures and body templates must be applied for and pass review before use; feedback normally arrives in about 2 hours after creating a signature or template (review hours Monday–Sunday 9:00–23:00). Domestic signatures run 2–12 characters, international/Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan signatures 2–15 characters; at most 50 signatures can be applied for; signatures are optional for international/Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan SMS; individual users can apply for signatures with supporting proof (app store backend screenshots for apps, ICP filing backend screenshots for websites, published and verifiable official account or mini program screenshots); signatures must identify the company or individual, with no neutral signatures. Variable format is {1}, with verification-code variables limited to 0–6 digit numbers. Carrier filing after signature approval generally takes 7–10 working days; only enterprise-verified users can send domestic marketing SMS. The review-standards PDF sections date from 2022 and are aging; the 2-hour review is confirmed again in 2026 text.

Billing splits and character cap

Mainland: total length of 70 characters or fewer bills as 1 message; longer messages split at 67 characters per message; a single send must not exceed 500 characters. International splitting rules — check the official page.

Overage and paid tiers

Packages last until used up; batch sends with insufficient balance automatically switch the excess to daily postpaid billing (once enabled). Further overage details are on the official page.

FAQ

Is the first-activation gift permanently free?

No. The gift is one-time: 200 messages for enterprises and 100 for individuals, valid 3 months, one per verified entity for first-activation accounts only.

Can individuals send marketing SMS?

No. Individual users may only send verification and notification messages; only enterprise-verified users can send domestic marketing SMS.

How long does signature review take?

Feedback normally arrives in about 2 hours, with review hours Monday–Sunday 9:00–23:00; carrier filing after signature approval generally takes 7–10 working days.
