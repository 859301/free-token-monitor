Plivo Verify charges zero fees for Fraud Shield and OTP verification services — only SMS, voice, or WhatsApp channel charges apply. Zero Verify fees

From the official Verify page: Zero charges for both Fraud Shield and OTP verification services. Only pay SMS, Voice or WhatsApp charges. A second wording on the same page: Plivo Verify charges only for the SMS — OTP delivery is included free.

US SMS prices and surcharges

Long and short codes $0.0077 per message (same for sending and receiving), toll-free $0.0079 per message; MMS long code $0.0180, toll-free and short code $0.0200. Carrier surcharges: AT&T $0.0035, T-Mobile sending $0.0045 / receiving $0.0025, Verizon sending $0.0050. Number rental: long code $0.50 per month, toll-free $1.00 per month, short code regular $500 per month and vanity $1,000 per month (billed quarterly), plus a $1,500 one-time fee.

Price to China

From the China pricing page: long-code sending $0.1082 per message, with China Mobile, China Telecom (CDMA), China Unicom, and China Others all at $0.1082 per message; receiving is Contact support.

Trial figures with source and scope notes

The US SMS pricing page footer only says receive trial credits and no credit card, with no amount. The $10 wording appears in the voice pricing page footer: Get $10 in free credits. No credit card required. The 14-day wording appears on a Voice AI Agents comparison page: 14-day free trial with $10 in credits. No card required. Whether the 14 days also bind the SMS trial allowance is not stated on the SMS page, so scope follows the source. The 3-week and no-expiry claims appear only in secondhand sources and are not used; the claim that SMS requires a $1,000/month Enterprise plan is likewise secondhand only and is not used, while the official FAQ states that signing up for a free account grants trial credits, with pay-as-you-go pricing, no monthly commitments or minimum fees, and no contract.

Overage and paid tiers

Pay-as-you-go pricing, no monthly commitments or minimum fees, billed per message, no contract (official FAQ wording). Further overage details are on the official page.

FAQ

Does Verify really charge no verification fee?

The official Verify page states zero charges for Fraud Shield and OTP verification services, with only SMS, voice, or WhatsApp channel charges applying.

Do the $10 credit and 14 days apply to the SMS trial?

The $10 figure appears in the voice pricing page footer and the 14-day figure on a Voice AI comparison page; whether the 14 days bind the SMS trial allowance is not stated on the SMS page — check the official page.

What does SMS to China cost?

Long-code sending is $0.1082 per message for all carriers; receiving is Contact support.
