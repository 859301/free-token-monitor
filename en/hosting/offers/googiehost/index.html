What the free tier includes

Per the official free page, the free tier includes 1000 MB NVMe SSD, unmetered bandwidth, a DirectAdmin panel, a free SSL certificate, 1 website with a free subdomain, 1 email account, 1 FTP account, a 400+ one-click app installer (including WordPress, Joomla, Drupal), and an AI site builder. An owned domain can be pointed at the account. The official site describes itself as operating since 2012, with 2.7 million+ websites and 98.95% uptime (vendor claims).

Database count: two pages disagree

The free page plan table writes “1 Email account + MySQL DB” (1 email account + 1 MySQL database). The PHP page FAQ writes “Create multiple databases”. The two pages disagree; the exact database count follows the official page.

Subdomain suffixes: three wordings

The free page writes free subdomain options such as whf.bz or cu.ma; the free page FAQ writes yourdomain.cu.ma or yourdomain.hf.bz; the PHP page writes the free subdomain as yourproject.googiehost.com. The three wordings disagree; the suffix actually offered in the account backend at signup applies.

PHP versions

The PHP page body text and FAQ state PHP 7.4, 8.0, 8.1, 8.2, 8.3, switchable in DirectAdmin; FTP + Cron Job are both included. The PHP page title states “PHP 8.4 Ready”, conflicting with the 8.3 ceiling in the body text.

Idle policy

The TOS (updated 2026-09-30) states: an account with 15 days without activity is suspended; if not restored within 22 days, it is terminated permanently; regular logins are required. Content backups are the account holder’s own job. The TOS also states: 18 years or older; a government-issued photo ID may be required on request.

Unconfirmed information

Hits/CPU caps: not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. The installer name Softaculous appears only in an image filename, unnamed in the body text. The free page’s own comparison table claims InfinityFree has forced ads, contradicting the InfinityFree official site.

How to claim

  1. Register on the GoogieHost free page
  2. Pick a free subdomain or point an owned domain
  3. Install WordPress with the one-click installer in DirectAdmin
  4. Free SSL activates automatically; log in regularly to stay active.
