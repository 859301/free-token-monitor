What the free tier includes

Per the official homepage, the free tier (x10 Basic) includes 500 MB starting space, 2 MySQL databases, 3 domains, 3 email accounts, a DirectAdmin panel, a 300+ app installer (including WordPress, Joomla, MyBB), FTP and file management, and site templates. The official site describes itself as operating since 2004. A free account is limited to 1 basic hosting service.

Staying active: 30 days / 5 days / 21 days

The TOS states: staying active requires logging in to the account portal at least every 30 days, keeping scripts updated, and making site-building progress within 5 days of signup; empty accounts older than 5 days are suspended. Idle accounts are removed from the server 21 days after suspension, and can be restored through the account portal within 21 days, with no manual step. Upgrading removes these limits. Another idle-day figure exists: second-hand reviews relay a June 2026 admin post mentioning 45 days, conflicting with the TOS figure of 30 days.

Files and backups

The TOS states: files larger than 10 MB are removed automatically for non-upgraded accounts. Backups are entirely the customer’s responsibility; accounts suspended for violations are not guaranteed a backup; suspension means immediate loss of file and database access. Providing an email address means agreeing to receive a limited amount of marketing email. High-resource sites can be limited automatically or suspended temporarily, with no specific CPU figure.

Unconfirmed information

Bandwidth/traffic figures, the specific PHP version, SSL certificates, and ad policy: not stated on the official homepage — check the official page. Second-hand claims state unlimited bandwidth, a Softaculous installer, and configurable SSL — none confirmed on the official pages read.
