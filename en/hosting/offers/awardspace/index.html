What the free tier includes

Per the official KB (page updated 2024-07-13), the free tier includes 1 GB storage, 5 GB traffic per month, about 5,000 visits/month, 1 MySQL database, and 1 email account. Per the official free WordPress page: 100% ad-free, “free lifetime WordPress hosting”, a free subdomain list, 1 domain + 3 subdomains, sharing 1 database for up to 4 websites, an in-house control panel, the Zacky installer, and a 1-hour support response (vendor claim).

Database and query limits

The TOS states: 1 MySQL database per customer, 30 MB of space in total; at most 12,000 MySQL queries per hour, at most 30 concurrent connections. The TOS also states: the free tier disables some PHP functions (allow_url_fopen, fsockopen, pfsockpen, getrusage, and others); some file types are banned (including .htacl, .avi, .mp3, .zip, .rar, .gz, .torrent, .exe, and others).

Idle rules and validity

The TOS states: a free account with 12 months of no traffic at all is marked idle; before marking it idle, the system sends 3 notices (5 days), then 3 more notices (5 days) after blocking, during which reactivation can be requested, otherwise the account and data are deleted permanently. The free service is valid for 1 year from signup, renewing automatically to the next year on a successful login; an account with no successful login for over 1 year counts as expired and may be closed. Automatic logins for the purpose of extending validity are not allowed. Accounts blocked for TOS violations are deleted within 7 days without notice.

PHP and SSL unconfirmed

PHP version: not stated on the official pages read. Second-hand claims conflict badly: old PHP 5/7 versions, PHP 7 only, measured PHP 8.4.25, PHP 8.4 + MySQL 5.7 — four claims side by side, unconfirmed. SSL: the official pages read do not state free-tier SSL; second-hand measurements state the free tier does not support SSL and has no usable HTTPS, unconfirmed on the official pages read. Card requirements: not stated on the official pages read; several second-hand claims state no credit card is needed, unconfirmed on the official pages read.
