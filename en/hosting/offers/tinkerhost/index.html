What the free tier includes

Per the official free page, the free tier includes 5 GiB disk, 100 GB bandwidth per month, a 30,000 inode cap, unlimited domain count, MySQL + phpMyAdmin, free SSL, Softaculous 300+, the Site.Pro builder, FTP and an online file manager, custom error pages, .htaccess support, CNAME, Cloudflare compatibility, a firewall, and AlmaLinux servers. The official site states accounts are set up within 5 minutes.

Terms: Byet/iFastNet terms apply

The TOS (v4, updated 2026-10-11) states: free hosting accounts (prefixes thsi_/tnkr_) follow the partner iFastNet/Byet Internet service terms (byet.host/terms-of-service). The Byet TOS gives no specific idle day count, only general terms; the TinkerHost idle policy likewise follows the official page.

Ads, stated both ways

The support page FAQ states: no house ads are placed on user sites. The TOS states: the service may include ads (ads inside the client area), removable through TinkerHost+ or a browser plugin. The free page writes “No more ad-ridden error pages” (custom error pages without ads).

Backup responsibility

The TOS states: backups are entirely the account holder’s responsibility, with no automatic backups; having a backup made on request costs $14.99 USD, waivable at discretion.
