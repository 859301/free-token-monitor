What the free tier includes

Per the official free page, the free tier includes 5 GB NVMe disk, Unlimited bandwidth, PHP 8.3 (Zend & ionCube), unlimited MySQL 8 / MariaDB 11.4 databases, phpMyAdmin, VistaPanel, Softaculous 400+ one-click apps, a free site builder with templates, custom error pages, password-protected folders, and query/disk/CPU statistics. Free subdomains come in 24 suffixes (such as 10001mb.com, 22web.org, is-great.net), or a domain can be brought.

SSL: subdomains and owned domains differ

The official free page states: HTTPS is included automatically on all free subdomains; with a brought domain, a free SSL certificate can be installed in the panel. The two flows differ: subdomains need zero configuration, owned domains need a manual panel step.

Account cap and backup responsibility

The TOS states: max 3 free accounts per person, and all accounts over the limit are terminated without notice. Backups are the customer’s responsibility; no free backup/restore is provided; having a backup created on request costs $9.99 USD, waivable at discretion. When an account is terminated, the password, all files, and all content are deleted with it.

Unconfirmed information

Hits/day cap: the full TOS gives no number — check the official page; the InfinityFree 50,000 hits/day figure cannot be applied to Byet. Specific idle day count: the TOS writes only about reserving the right to set an idle retention day count and to log out long-idle accounts, with no day count. Cron: claims conflict — the Byet free page advertises cron-related features, while InfinityFree states a platform-level cron ban; the two disagree. Inode cap: the TOS states 180,000 for premium only, with no free-tier figure. Relationship with InfinityFree: belonging to the same iFastNet ecosystem is a second-hand claim; supporting evidence is the Byet TOS privacy policy link pointing to ifastnet.com.
