What the free tier includes

Per the official homepage and Features page, the free tier includes 5 GB disk, Unlimited bandwidth, 10 MySQL databases, 10 addon domains, 10 parked domains, single file max 10 MB, PHP up to 8.4, MySQL 8.0 / MariaDB 11.4, .htaccess, a Softaculous installer, Cron Jobs, phpMyAdmin, email accounts, Cloudflare support, and a firewall. The official site describes itself as 12+ years old, with 300,000+ websites and 99.9% uptime (vendor claims).

SSL: self-signed

The Features page writes “Automatic Self Signed SSL” and “Own SSL certificate”. A self-signed certificate differs from a CA-issued certificate: browsers may show a security warning on access; whether it is a trusted CA certificate is not stated on the official site — check the official page.
