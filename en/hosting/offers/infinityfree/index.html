What the free tier includes

Per the official homepage, the free tier includes 5 GB disk space, Unlimited bandwidth, PHP 8.4, MySQL 8.0 / MariaDB 11.4, 400 MySQL databases, a free SSL certificate, free subdomains (25+ suffixes) or a brought domain, a free DNS service, and full .htaccess support. Softaculous installs 400+ scripts with one click (including WordPress), and the Site.pro builder builds sites without code. The official site describes itself as operating since 2012, with 500,000+ websites and 99.9% uptime (vendor claims, unmeasured).

The hits cap: 50,000/day

The official forum KB states: a hit is every request for a website file, not a page view, visit, or visitor. A page with 2 CSS + 3 JS + 5 images produces 11 hits on one load. InfinityFree caps hits at 50,000 per day, counted on a rolling 24-hour reset (not necessarily at midnight); at the cap, the account goes offline for 24 hours, then recovers automatically. The free-tier hits quota cannot be raised on request. Background requests (such as chat scripts) count as hits. Analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) count page views only, so the numbers run lower than hits usage.

Idle and deletion

The official forum KB states: a site needs at least 5 hits in 30 days, or it is suspended as idle, and can be reactivated by clicking Reactivate in the client area. The deletion timing for suspended accounts differs between two KB passages: one writes “Suspended accounts are deleted automatically after 20–60 days”, the other writes only “deleted after some time” for deactivated accounts, with no day count. The official homepage FAQ also states: the only requirement is a small number of hits per month for the site; no login or forum posting is required.

Unconfirmed information

Panel type: not named on the official homepage — check the official page. Email accounts, cron, CPU figures, SSH: not stated on the official pages read. Second-hand claims state no email accounts, cron disabled since 2023-08-09, and CPU figures kept confidential — none confirmed on the official pages read. PHP version: the official homepage shows PHP 8.4, while second-hand reviews from August–September 2026 state PHP 8.3; the two disagree.

Cloudflare cannot be used on InfinityFree free subdomains, but can be used on an owned domain (official forum guide).
