Three servers, stated separately

Johnny: the free server; free signup reopened, but slots fill up fast. Tommy: the donor server; a one-time $2 donation opens an account at any time, with no monthly fee after that; different donation amounts map to different storage (up to 7 GB). Morty: the elastic server, from $1/month; a positive account balance is enough, with no login requirement; over the cap, Morty is charged rather than suspended. At most 1 account across Tommy and Johnny combined; multiple Morty accounts need different email addresses. The service is provided as-is, with no availability guarantee (TOS).

Load cap

The Wiki states: shared servers run under caps of 200 GB of memory per day and 10,000 CPU per day; Tommy/Johnny accounts are suspended over the cap, Morty is charged. Load is sampled every 60 seconds: 1 MB of memory used for a full 24 hours totals 1.4 GB per day; the CPU unit is 1% of single-core speed, and 1% sustained for a full day totals 1440. Cron count is unlimited but counts toward total load; a cron running every 1 minute easily exceeds the cap. Load figures can be viewed on the load page inside the panel. An old wiki revision summary states 100 GB/day; the current page read on the day states 200 GB/day.

Idle rules and renewal

The Wiki states: Tommy/Johnny accounts log in at heliohost.org or heliohost.org/login at least every 30 days, and each login keeps the account active for another 30 days; a warning email goes out at 28 days, and the account goes offline at 30 days; activation at heliohost.org/renew takes effect within 2 hours at most (an Apache restart is needed). Logging in to Plesk alone may not count: the Wiki states login must happen at heliohost.org or /login, and other login methods are not supported. Morty has no login requirement.

Slots and signup

The signup page states: the Johnny server rebuild is complete, free signup is open again, and slots fill up fast. The Wiki FAQ states: Johnny free slots reset every 12 hours (UTC midnight and noon). The TOS states: at least 13 years old, no disposable email addresses; Johnny/Tommy are limited to non-commercial use (hobby, development, registered nonprofits, personal sites; startup micro-businesses acceptable but reviewed). Duplicate accounts account for about 60% of suspensions in the past year (Wiki).

Backup responsibility

The Wiki FAQ states: after an account is deleted, the data cannot be recovered — HelioHost keeps no user data backups and takes no backup responsibility; backups must be made regularly and downloaded locally. The Wiki also has a backup tutorial page.

Unconfirmed information

SSL, card requirements, ads, domain counts: not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. Second-hand claims: Johnny at most 5 domains, PHP 5.6–8.5 switchable, Let’s Encrypt SSL, a 97% uptime target (not an SLA) — none confirmed on the official pages read.

FAQ

What is the difference between Johnny and Tommy?

Johnny is the free server, with limited slots released every 12 hours; Tommy is the donor server, where a one-time $2 donation opens an account at any time, with no monthly fee after that. Both have 1000 MB storage, the same load cap, 1 account per person, non-commercial use only, and a 30-day login renewal. Morty starts at $1/month, has no login requirement, and is charged over the cap. Verified 2026-11-13.

How are Johnny slots claimed?

The Wiki FAQ states: Johnny free slots reset every 12 hours (UTC midnight and noon). The signup page states slots fill up fast. Signup requires age 13 or older, with no disposable email addresses. Verified 2026-11-13.

What happens over the load cap?

The Wiki states: over 200 GB memory/day + 10,000 CPU/day, Tommy/Johnny accounts are suspended; Morty is charged. Load is sampled every 60 seconds, cron counts toward total load, and the figures can be viewed on the load page inside the panel. Verified 2026-11-13.
