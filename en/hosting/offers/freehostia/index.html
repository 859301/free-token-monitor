What the free tier includes

Per the official plan page, the Chocolate free plan includes 250 MB disk, 6 GB traffic per month, 5 hosted domains, 1 MySQL v.5 database (10 MB storage), 3 email accounts, a Let’s Encrypt SSL certificate, a 1-click installer, a multilingual control panel, 24/7 support with a 1-hour ticket response (vendor claims). The plan page states: usable for any length of time at no charge. The plan page general features area shows a “30 Day Free Trial” label; per the TOS, the 30-day trial applies to paid plans.

1-year validity and renewal

The TOS states: free accounts default to 1 year validity, renewable free of charge. Shared accounts are valid for 1 year, renewable. Bandwidth is 6 GB, with overage at $12/GB/year prepaid; subdomains number 15, with overage at $12/year.

File size: two wordings disagree

The plan page spec table writes File Size Limit 2MB. The TOS writes free plan customers may not upload files larger than 500KB. The two wordings disagree; the TOS is the terms document agreed to at signup.

CPU and outbound connections

The TOS states: the CPU cap is 5% (longest 180 seconds), and the Chocolate plan 1.3% within 24 hours. Outbound connections are disabled for free accounts. No IP address is included. SMTP is off; only IMAP/POP3 mail retrieval is supported.
