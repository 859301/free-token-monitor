What the free tier includes

Per the official free page, the free tier includes 10 GB storage, unmetered bandwidth, 1 website, 1 MySQL database, 1 email account, Apache + PHP (.htaccess, 256 MB PHP memory, 200 MB uploads, 60-second execution time), a site builder (170+ templates), the Apps Installer, and the Hosting Control Panel. The vendor states PHP sites “typically handle around 30,000 daily visitors” (vendor claim). Free sites are online 24/7, with no time-of-day limits.

An owned domain is required

The official free page FAQ asks, in the original wording: “Can I host a site on a free subdomain?” — the answer: No; the service requires a self-controlled registered domain. The free page states: sites on free subdomains such as user.somedomain.tld are not hosted; free domains such as .TK, .ML, .GA are not accepted. An owned domain points to NS1.FREEHOSTING.COM and NS2.FREEHOSTING.COM.

SSL and add-on prices

One-time add-ons on the free page: SSL (HTTPS + free SSL certificate) $29.95; app mail enabling $9.95; +1 mailbox $14.95; +3 mailboxes $34.95; +1 MySQL database $24.95; +3 MySQL databases $49.95; +1 subdomain $14.95; +3 subdomains $34.95; PHP version selector $9.95; scheduled tasks (Crontab) $19.95. Script outgoing mail is disabled by default; enabling it needs an add-on or a paid subscription.

Idle policy

The TOS states: when a free account’s domain goes 90 days in a row without resolving to the server IP or without visitors, it counts as idle, and FreeHosting.com reserves the right to remove the account without prior notice. The Fair Use Policy states: a mailbox unvisited for 180 days may be deleted along with its contents; hosting space is only for files needed to run the website, not as a file backup store or file exchange service; user-generated backups must be downloaded locally promptly and deleted from the server.

FAQ

Can FreeHosting.com be used without a domain?

No. The official free page FAQ states: the service requires a self-controlled registered domain; free subdomains are not hosted; free domains such as .TK/.ML/.GA are not accepted. Hosts with free subdomains include InfinityFree, Byet.host, and GoogieHost. Verified 2026-11-13.

How does HTTPS work on FreeHosting.com?

The free tier includes no free SSL: HTTPS + a free SSL certificate is a $29.95 one-time add-on. The PHP version selector is $9.95 and scheduled tasks are $19.95, both one-time add-ons. Verified 2026-11-13.

What happens after 90 days without visitors?

The TOS states: a domain that goes 90 days in a row without resolving to the server IP or without visitors counts as idle, and FreeHosting.com reserves the right to remove the account without prior notice. Verified 2026-11-13.
