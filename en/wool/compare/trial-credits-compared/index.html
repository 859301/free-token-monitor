Trial credits compared: Vultr / DigitalOcean / Akamai / Hetzner

Updated 2026-11-13 · Data from vendor official pages

Short answer: none of the 4 trial credits is unconditional free money: Vultr’s match requires a deposit first with 50/50 offset; Hetzner has the clearest rules but the credit only lasts the current month; Akamai’s $100 is solid but the trial length is unstated; DigitalOcean’s $200 is the biggest but the credited amount is disputed. Which to pick depends on what you want: clearest rules → Hetzner (redeem on the 1st); biggest amount → Akamai (confirm the day count first); longest slow use → Vultr match (12 months); DigitalOcean — verify the credited amount first.

Card comparison

DigitalOcean and Akamai explicitly require a card / payment method; Vultr matching requires a deposit first (which means paying); Hetzner’s promo page does not state it. Card-based trials all carry auto-charge risk: deal with your machines before the trial ends.

Expiry comparison

The four expiry rules all differ: Vultr match expires after 12 months; Hetzner expires at month end; Akamai expires when the trial period ends (day count unknown); DigitalOcean runs 60 days (campaign page). Common ground: none of them rolls over or re-issues unused credits. Confirm the expiry time before claiming, and set a reminder after claiming.

FAQ

Which credit is the most solid?

Hetzner has the clearest rules (valid in the current month; redeeming on the 1st gets the most value); Akamai’s $100 is the biggest solid amount (but the trial length is unstated); Vultr’s match lasts longest (12 months) but requires a deposit first; DigitalOcean’s $200 is disputed — verify the credited amount first.

Can I claim several at once?

Yes, separately: each vendor only limits its own new customers, and credits only work at that vendor — they are not transferable. When claiming several, note each vendor’s expiry time separately.
