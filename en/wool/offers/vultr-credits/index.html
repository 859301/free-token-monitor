Deposit-match rules

The official Match page’s rule, in its own wording: Vultr matches your first deposit dollar-for-dollar, up to $100, for newly opened accounts only. The page offers $10, $25, $50, and $100 deposit amounts; after choosing an amount and clicking purchase, the matched credit is issued (page example: deposit $100, get $100 in bonus credit, account balance $200).

Three key rules come from the official Match page FAQ: first, matched credits expire 12 months after issuance; second, credits offset 50/50 — on $10 of accumulated hourly billing, $5 comes from promo credits and $5 from real funds; third, the offer is limited-time, but the page only says “available for a limited time”, with no end date stated. By the 50/50 rule, using up $100 of matched credits requires spending $100 of real funds at the same time.

$200/$300/$250 free credits

The official promo page lists three tiers of free credits side by side: “Try Vultr for free with $200 / $300 / $250 free credit.” The footnote under each tier is the same: promo credits apply only to selected products, cannot be combined with other offers, restrictions and terms apply, new customers only.

The promo page does not state the validity of these three tiers, nor the differences between them (for example, whether they correspond to different campaign entries). After claiming, confirm the credit expiry time in your account — do not plan around any third-party day counts.

Requirements & limits

  • New customers only: the Match offer is only for users first coming to Vultr Cloud; existing customers are not eligible, and neither are duplicate accounts.
  • Selected products only: promo credits cannot be used for all products; the exact scope is per the official page.
  • Cannot be combined with other offers.
  • Matching requires a deposit first: this is not a purely free credit — you spend real funds first, and Vultr matches the same amount.

After credits run out / expire

Matched credits expire 12 months after issuance, and any unused remainder is lost; afterwards, usage is billed at normal prices from real funds. Expiry rules for the $200/$300/$250 credits are as shown in the account. Credits are not a long-term free tier — nothing is re-issued automatically at expiry.

Notes

  • Promo codes: the official promo page and Match page bodies give no promo-code string to copy; code strings circulating from third parties were not verified on official pages, and If a page asks for a promo code, go by what the official page shows.
  • Limited-time with no end date stated: the offer may end at any time; confirm the official page still shows it before claiming.
  • 50/50 offset is easy to underestimate: work out what your machine costs per hour first, then decide how much to deposit.

How to claim

  1. Open a new account on the Vultr website (new customers only; duplicate accounts are not eligible).
  2. On the official promo page, confirm the current match offer and free credits are still running, and check whether the page states an end date.
  3. Deposit ($10 / $25 / $50 / $100 tiers) or claim free credits per the official page instructions, and confirm the credit expiry time in your account.
