What the free plan includes

Per the pricing page, the Free plan $0/month: 3,000 emails/month, 100 emails/day, 3 domains, 3 inboxes, 30-day data retention, 5 AI credits, 10,000 automation runs, SSO, ticket support. The daily and monthly numbers both appear on the pricing page; how the two caps interact is covered in Daily vs monthly limits.

Permanence

The pricing page FAQ, answering whether a free trial exists, states a free-forever plan is offered. The free plan is not a trial, and no trial-period wording applies.

Overage behavior

Pay-as-you-go is only available on paid plans. Without pay-as-you-go enabled, exceeding the limit triggers a notification, with an option to upgrade (FAQ wording).

Counting rules

The FAQ states: received emails count toward the transactional quota; Broadcast emails do not count toward the transactional limit. Transactional email and Broadcast email are metered under different rules.

Paid plans

Pro $20/month for 50,000 emails; Scale $90/month for 100,000 emails; overage $0.90/1,000 emails. Other paid details — check the official page.
