What the free plan includes

The official help center free plan page states: 300 emails sent per day; up to 100,000 contacts; 1 user; transactional email retry queue 1,000; automation 2,000 contacts; 50 open deals / 1 pipeline. The pricing page states "Free forever, no credit card needed".

Daily reset, no rollover

The sending allowance is 300 emails per day, reset daily, with no rollover: allowance unused on one day does not carry to the next day. For daily metering, see Daily vs monthly limits.

Branding

Emails sent on the free plan always carry the "Sent with Brevo" branding. Branding rules after upgrading — check the official page.

Paid plans

Starter from $9/month, Standard from $18/month, Professional from $499/month, Enterprise custom pricing; prices exclude tax; annual billing at 9 tenths of the monthly price. Included volumes for each paid plan — check the official page.
