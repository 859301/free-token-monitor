What the free plan includes

Per the pricing page, the Free Developer plan $0: 100 emails/month. The pricing page states the plan "never expires or runs out"; the FAQ states "Use it for however long you need, it doesn't expire."

No overages

The pricing page states "No overages allowed in this plan": the Free Developer plan does not allow overages.

No rollover and the tier gap

The FAQ states unused allowance does not roll over ("the unused emails do not roll over"). The FAQ also states there is no intermediate tier between 100 emails/month (Free Developer) and 10,000 emails/month. The FAQ states there is no annual plan ("Not right now").

Does the Postmark free plan expire?

No. The pricing page states "never expires or runs out"; the FAQ states it can be used for however long needed, it does not expire. Verified 2026-11-13.

What happens when the Postmark free plan exceeds 100 emails?

No overages are allowed. The pricing page states "No overages allowed in this plan". Verified 2026-11-13.
