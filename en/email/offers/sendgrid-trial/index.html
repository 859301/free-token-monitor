SendGrid trial

What the trial includes

Free Trial $0, 60 days, 100 emails/day; "no credit card required, no commitment"; 1 teammate, 1 event webhook, 3 days of searchable email activity history.

No permanent free plan

The pricing page has no permanent free plan, only the trial. The free plan wording in the page marketing copy refers to the same 60-day trial: the plan header reads "Free Trial $0/mo for 60 days".

Paid plans and tier prices

Per the pricing page: Essentials from $19.95/month, with the FAQ stating suitability for 50,000–100,000 emails per month; Pro from $89.95/month, including a dedicated IP, subuser management, SSO, and 7 days of activity history; Premier custom pricing. Tier prices in the official pricing PDF:

Old permanent free plan retired

FAQ

Does SendGrid still have a free plan?

No permanent free plan — only a 60-day free trial: 100 emails/day, no credit card required, no commitment. The free plan wording on the page refers to the same 60-day trial. Verified 2026-11-13.

What does the SendGrid trial include?

Free Trial $0, 60 days, 100 emails/day; 1 teammate, 1 event webhook, 3 days of searchable email activity history. Verified 2026-11-13.

Why does SendGrid show two different overage rates?

The official pricing PDF and the pricing web page show different overage rates: in the PDF, Essentials is $0.00133/email and $0.00088/email, and the five Pro tiers run $0.00110 to $0.00047/email; the web page shows Essentials $0.00075/email and Pro $0.000575/email.
