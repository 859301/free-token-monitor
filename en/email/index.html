Email: 3 free tiers, 1 entry not stated as permanent, and 1 60-day trial

Updated 2026-11-14 · 5 entries (verified 2026-11-13) · Specs and terms transcribed from vendor official pages

3 free tiers: Resend, Brevo, and Postmark. 1 entry not stated as permanent on the official site: Mailgun. 1 trial: SendGrid (60 days, 100 emails/day, no permanent free plan). Limit metering differs: Resend has both a daily and a monthly cap, Mailgun, Brevo, and the SendGrid trial are metered daily, and Postmark is metered monthly — see each detail page.

Email here means sending transactional email through an email API: signup verification, password resets, and order notifications. Broadcast and marketing email may be metered differently (Resend Broadcast email does not count toward the transactional limit — see Resend free tier). Domain verification (SPF/DKIM) is a general step before sending; domain and DNS options are on Domains & DNS.
