Firebase free allowances

Firebase · Verified 2026-11-13 · Page updated 2026-11-14

Full-stack platform Includes auth allowance Spark: overage disables the app Blaze: no hard cap possible

Firebase no-cost allowances span multiple products: Firestore 1 GiB storage, 10 GiB outbound, 20K writes / 50K reads / 20K deletes per day; Auth 50K MAU; Hosting 10 GB storage + 360 MB transfer per day; Realtime Database 100 concurrent connections, 1 GB storage, 360 MB downloads per day. Spark and Blaze differ in meaning: Spark is purely free, has hard limits, and disables the app on overage; Blaze is pay-as-you-go plus equivalent daily-calculated free allowances, requires a billing account, and cannot cap usage. Cloud Functions and Cloud Storage free allowances sit in the Blaze column; the Spark column reads Not applicable. Verified 2026-11-13 — check the official page before claiming.

Spark vs Blaze

Spark: purely free, hard limits, overage disables the app. Official FAQ wording: when you exceed any plan limit in any month, your app will be turned off. Blaze: pay-as-you-go + equivalent daily-calculated free allowances. Official FAQ wording: No-cost usage on the Blaze plan is calculated daily. Blaze requires a billing account and cannot cap usage. Official FAQ wording: No, you cannot currently cap your Blaze plan usage.

Hosting: 360 MB/day

Firebase Hosting free transfer is 360 MB per day, storage 10 GB, overage $0.15/GB. 10 GB/month is the equivalent expression for Realtime Database downloads (360 MB/day is about 10 GB/month), not a Hosting allowance; the two must not be mixed. For the deploy-side comparison, see Deploy & hosting.

Functions and Storage plan placement

Cloud Functions free allowances (2M invocations/month, 400K GB-s, 200K CPU-s, 5 GB outbound) sit in the Blaze column; the Spark column reads Not applicable; Cloud Functions needs a billing account. Cloud Storage free allowances likewise sit in the Blaze column; the Spark column reads Not applicable.
