What the free tier includes

Per the official pricing page (page note: updated 2026-11-04), Free $0 includes: 100 projects, 100 CU-hrs/project/month, 1 GB storage/project, max 2 CU (8 GB RAM), 5 GB object storage, Managed Better Auth 60k MAU/project, 5 GB egress/project, 6-hour/1 GB history window, 1 snapshot.

Permanent, no card

Official pricing page wording: Build and learn free with no time limits and no credit card required. Official FAQ wording: The Free plan is permanent, not a trial, and doesn’t require a credit card.

Scale to zero and overage

Scales to zero after 5 minutes idle. On overage, data is kept and compute pauses until the next cycle. Scale to zero is idle sleep, not deletion: data is kept, compute pauses.

Old numbers superseded

A Neon blog post dated 2026-11-02 wrote 50 CU-hours / 0.5 GB; the pricing page dated 2026-11-04 states 100 CU-hrs / 1 GB. Where an article shows the old numbers, the pricing page figures apply.
