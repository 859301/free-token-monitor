Free databases: 6 free database and data-layer allowances

Updated 2026-11-14 · 6 entries (verified 2026-11-13) · Specs and terms transcribed from vendor official pages

6 free database and data-layer allowances: Supabase, Neon, Turso, Upstash Redis, MongoDB Atlas, Firebase. Neon and Turso state no credit card on the official site; Neon states the Free plan is permanent, not a trial, and MongoDB Atlas M0 states Free forever. Supabase projects pause after 1 week of inactivity; Neon scales to zero after 5 minutes idle. PlanetScale Hobby is retired — see the exclusion box below.

Four questions decide a free database choice: whether it pauses or deletes data, whether the allowance is enough, whether overage stops the service or starts charging, and latency from China. Pause rules, card requirements, and overage behavior for all 6 entries follow each vendor’s official site; verification dates and official links are in the tables.

Services not in the list, and why

The following services are not in the tables above, with the reason for each stated as a fact.

  • PlanetScale Hobby: retirement announced 2024-03-06, retired 2024-04-08, no free tier.
  • Heroku free tier: removed 2022-11-28; now Eco from $5/month.
  • Cloudflare D1: seen only as a second-hand entry in a forum tool table (“Cloudflare D1 5GB”); current vendor official pricing terms unconfirmed.
  • db4free, freedb.tech, sqlpub, Divio: seen only in a forum free-resources thread; current vendor official terms unconfirmed.
  • PocketBase: self-hosted software, not a hosted free allowance.
  • Render free Postgres: 1 per workspace, 1 GB, expiring 30 days after creation, with a 14-day grace period after expiry, then deleted along with the data; part of a deploy service — see Render free tier.
  • Clerk, Auth0: authentication services, not databases.
