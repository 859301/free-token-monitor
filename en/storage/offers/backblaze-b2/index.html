Backblaze B2 (pay-as-you-go)

Backblaze · Verified 2026-11-13 · Page updated 2026-11-14

Pay-as-you-go No free tier Egress 3x rule Partner CDN egress free

Backblaze B2 is pay-as-you-go, with no free tier. Storage costs $6.95/TB/30 days; egress is free up to 3x average monthly stored data, $0.01/GB beyond that; egress via partner CDNs (Fastly, Cloudflare, bunny.net, CacheFly, CoreWeave, Equinix Metal, Vultr, phoenixNAP) is unlimited free; Overdrive costs $15/TB/month. Verified 2026-11-13 — check the official page before claiming.

FAQ

Does Backblaze B2 have a free tier?

No. The official pricing page showed no “10GB free” wording in either of two captures. B2 is pay-as-you-go: storage $6.95/TB/30 days. Verified 2026-11-13.

How is B2 egress charged?

Direct egress is free up to 3x average monthly stored data, $0.01/GB beyond that. Egress via partner CDNs is unlimited free. Verified 2026-11-13.

Which partner CDNs give free B2 egress?

Fastly, Cloudflare, bunny.net, CacheFly, CoreWeave, Equinix Metal, Vultr, phoenixNAP. Egress via these partner CDNs is unlimited free. Verified 2026-11-13.
