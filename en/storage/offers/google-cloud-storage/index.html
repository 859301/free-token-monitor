Google Cloud Storage free tier

Google Cloud · Verified 2026-11-14 · Page updated 2026-11-14

Free tier No end date Three US regions only Billing account required

Google Cloud Storage free tier, each month: 5 GB-months of regional storage (US regions only), 5,000 Class A operations, 50,000 Class B operations, 100 GB of outbound data transfer from North America (excluding China and Australia destinations). Free Tier benefits apply only to usage in the us-east1, us-west1, and us-central1 regions, with usage calculations combined across these regions. The Free Tier has no end date, but Google reserves the right to change or eliminate the offering with 30 days' advance notice; unused allowances do not roll over. A Google Cloud billing account is required to access the Free Tier. The $300 trial credit is valid for 90 days and is separate from the Free Tier. What the free tier includes

The Cloud Storage row of the official Free Tier documentation states: 5 GB-months of regional storage (US regions only) per month, corresponding to the storage of 5 GB of data for a period of 1 month; 5,000 Class A operations per month; 50,000 Class B operations per month; 100 GB of outbound data transfer from North America to all region destinations (excluding China and Australia) per month. The official product page pricing table also states: unlimited free operations, no retrieval fee for standard storage, unlimited inbound data transfer, and unlimited data transfer within the same location.

Regions covered

The official Free Tier documentation states: “The Free Tier benefits for Cloud Storage apply only to usage in the us-east1, us-west1, and us-central1 regions. Usage calculations are combined across these regions.” Usage in other regions falls outside the Free Tier.

Free Tier vs the trial

The Free Tier and the $300 trial credit are two different things. The Free Tier is a monthly allowance with no end date; the trial gives new customers a $300 Welcome credit valid for 90 days. With a Free Trial billing account, usage beyond the Free Tier limits is paid for by the $300 credit; with a paid billing account, usage beyond the Free Tier limits is billed at standard rates. If the trial ends without an upgrade to a paid billing account, the billing account is closed and resources are stopped, then permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period.

Egress rule

100 GB of outbound data transfer from North America to all region destinations (excluding China and Australia) is free each month. Inbound data transfer is unlimited, and data transfer within the same location is unlimited. Beyond the free allowance, general data transfer starts at $0.08/GiB.
