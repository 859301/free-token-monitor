UploadThing 2GB App free plan

UploadThing is a file upload service for developers. The 2GB App plan costs $0/month; the 100GB App plan costs $10/month and includes 100GB of storage; the Usage Based plan starts at $25/month, includes 250GB of storage, and charges $0.08/GB beyond 250GB. What kind of service it is

The official homepage is headed “Better file uploads for developers” and presents UploadThing as the easier (and safer) alternative to S3 for developers (official wording: “Developers deserve better than S3”). UploadThing provides an upload button and server routes, with files uploaded and managed through its service — a different form from object storage operated directly through an S3 bucket API.

Paid-plan feature rows

The 100GB App feature row lists: 100GB of storage, 30 days of audit log retention, Regions, Private Files. The Usage Based feature row lists: 250GB of included storage, 30 days of audit log retention, $0.08 per GB over 250GB, Regions, Private Files. Regions and Private Files appear in the paid-plan feature rows; whether the free plan includes them is not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.
