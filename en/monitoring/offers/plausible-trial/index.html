A trial, not a free tier

Terms of service (last updated September 2026) wording: “After the trial period, you need to pay in advance to keep using the service.” (After the trial period ends, prepayment is needed to keep using the service.) The 30-day free trial needs no credit card; the docs and the terms of service agree.

Plan structure

Starter: 1 website. Growth: up to 3 websites. Business: up to 10 websites, including funnels, user journeys, revenue tracking, custom properties, Stats API. Plans start at $9/month; annual billing saves 2 months. Specific prices per tier — check the official page.

Overage rules

The docs state: no surprise charges; exceeding the tier for two consecutive months triggers a notice asking for an upgrade.

Self-hosting

Official page wording: “Plausible Community Edition is the free, self-hostable version.” The code is open source, AGPLv3; the tracking script is MIT licensed. The terms of service state self-hosted use is subject to the applicable open-source license.
