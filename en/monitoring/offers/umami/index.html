What Hobby includes and excludes

Included: 100,000 events, 3 websites, 6-month data retention, full reporting suite, data export, community support. Not included: team members, API, email reports, session replay, heatmap.

FAQ

How many websites does Umami Hobby include?

3 websites, 100,000 events, 6-month data retention. Verified 2026-11-13.

What happens when Umami exceeds its event allowance?

Paid-plan overage is billed per event, data collection is not interrupted, and no data is lost. The Hobby Additional events cell is empty, with no per-event price; Hobby overage behavior is not stated on the official pages read. Verified 2026-11-13.

Can Umami be self-hosted?

Yes. Umami is open source, MIT license, free to self-host. Verified 2026-11-13.
