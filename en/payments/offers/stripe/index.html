Standard processing fees

The standard processing fee is 2.9% + 30¢ per successful transaction (US domestic cards), with international cards adding 1.5%. No setup fee, no monthly fee. The dispute fee is $15 per dispute.

Managed Payments (Merchant of Record)

Managed Payments is Stripe’s Merchant of Record product, priced at 3.5% per successful transaction, plus Payments fees. Per the pricing page, it includes indirect tax compliance in 75+ countries, fraud prevention, dispute management, invoicing, and customer support. A Merchant of Record acts as the seller of record in a transaction, taking on tax, fraud protection, refund, and related responsibilities; payment processing alone only processes payments.

Supported-country list: mainland China is not on it

The Stripe official supported country/region list has 50 entries; mainland China is not on the list. Stripe does not support direct signup for mainland China entities. Hong Kong is on the list. India and Indonesia are marked Preview. Côte d’Ivoire, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, and South Africa are marked Extended network (via Paystack). Signup eligibility follows the official supported-country list.

Stripe Atlas

Stripe Atlas costs $500 one-time, including government fees and first-year registered agent service. The official supported-country page states Atlas can register a US company from anywhere. Specific application conditions and procedures: check the official page.

Billing and Tax

Stripe Billing is usage-based at 0.7%, with annual plans from $620/month. Stripe Tax: no-code integration 0.5% per transaction, API integration $0.50 per transaction (registered tax regions).

Unconfirmed information

Whether signup requires a card, and what verification documents are required, are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. Signup eligibility for mainland China entities follows the official supported-country list.

FAQ

Can an individual in mainland China open a Stripe account directly?

No. Mainland China is not on Stripe’s official supported country/region list (50 entries), and Stripe does not support direct signup for mainland China entities. Hong Kong is on the list. Atlas can register a US company from anywhere, $500 one-time. Signup eligibility follows the official supported-country list. Verified 2026-11-13.

What is the Stripe Managed Payments fee?

3.5% per successful transaction, plus Payments fees. Includes indirect tax compliance in 75+ countries, fraud prevention, dispute management, invoicing, and customer support. Verified 2026-11-13.

Does Stripe charge a monthly fee?

Standard processing has no setup fee and no monthly fee. Stripe Billing is usage-based at 0.7%, with annual plans from $620/month. Verified 2026-11-13.
