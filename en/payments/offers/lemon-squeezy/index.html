Lemon Squeezy fees

Lemon Squeezy · Verified 2026-11-13 · Page updated 2026-11-14

Merchant of Record No monthly fee No card required at signup Migration target announced

Lemon Squeezy e-commerce costs 5% + 50¢ per transaction, with no monthly fee. It is a Merchant of Record, including sales tax/VAT collection, remittance, and filing; AI fraud protection; license keys; and subscription billing. It supports PayPal and other payment methods; email marketing is $0/month, free for up to 500 subscribers; signup needs no card. The 2026 Update official blog (2026-01-28) states the team is building Stripe Managed Payments, with the goal of providing a migration path; the blog states no closing date and no fee rate. Verified 2026-11-13 — check the official page before signing up.

Fees

The e-commerce fee is 5% + 50¢ per transaction, with no monthly fee. Supports PayPal and other payment methods.

Services included as Merchant of Record

Lemon Squeezy is a Merchant of Record, including sales tax/VAT collection, remittance, and filing; AI fraud protection; license keys; and subscription billing.

Email marketing

Email marketing is $0/month, free for up to 500 subscribers. Pricing above 500 subscribers: check the official page.

Additional fees

The pricing page states: Some payments may be subject to additional fees. Transactions outside the US may carry a small additional fee; details are on the docs page — check the official page.

Migration status: what the official blog states

The 2026 Update official blog (co-founder JR Farr, 2026-01-28) states the team is building a new Merchant of Record product, Stripe Managed Payments, for Stripe, at the cost of slower support responses and less frequent product updates. Goal wording: provide Lemon Squeezy users an easy way to migrate to Stripe Managed Payments. At the time, Managed Payments supported merchants in 35+ countries, with public access to be announced; merchants outside Stripe-supported countries were pointed to Atlas. The full blog states no closing date and no fee rate.

Unconfirmed information

Supported countries, verification requirements, and payout routes and settlement methods are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. Post-migration fees follow the Stripe official pricing page.

FAQ

Does Lemon Squeezy charge a monthly fee?

No. The e-commerce fee is 5% + 50¢ per transaction, with no monthly fee. Email marketing is $0/month, free for up to 500 subscribers. Verified 2026-11-13.

Will Lemon Squeezy shut down?

The 2026 Update official blog states no closing date. The blog states the team is building Stripe Managed Payments for Stripe, with the goal of providing a migration path. Verified 2026-11-13.

What fee applies after migrating to Stripe Managed Payments?

The blog states no fee rate. Post-migration fees follow the Stripe official pricing page; the Stripe pricing page states Managed Payments is 3.5% + Payments fees. Verified 2026-11-13.
