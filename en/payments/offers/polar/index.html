Polar fees

Polar · Verified 2026-11-13 · Page updated 2026-11-14

Merchant of Record Four pricing tiers Disputes $15 each

Polar is a Merchant of Record with four pricing tiers: Starter free at 5% + 50¢ per transaction with standard support; Pro $20/month at 3.8% + 40¢ with priority support, breaking even at about $1,379/month in sales; Growth $100/month at 3.6% + 35¢ with priority support, about $5,634/month; Scale $400/month at 3.4% + 30¢ with Slack + priority support, about $19,048/month. International cards add 1.5%, and disputes cost $15 each. Verified 2026-11-13 — check the official page before signing up.

Additional fees

International cards (non-US cards) add 1.5%. Disputes cost $15 each, regardless of outcome.

Payout fees

Payout fees pass through Stripe fees with no markup: $2 per month with payouts, 0.25% + $0.25 per payout, currency conversion 0.25% (EU)–1%.

Early Member rule

Organizations created before 2026-05-27 permanently keep the 4% + 40¢ + subscription fee +0.5% rate. Upgrading to a paid plan permanently loses that rate; dropping back to Starter uses the new 5% + 50¢ rate.

FAQ

What are Polar’s four break-even points?
