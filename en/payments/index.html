Payments: 1 payment processor and 3 Merchant of Record fee comparisons

Updated 2026-11-14 · 4 entries (verified 2026-11-13) · Fees and terms transcribed from vendor official pages

4 entries: Stripe (payment processing, standard fee 2.9% + 30¢, US domestic cards, international cards +1.5%, no monthly fee; Managed Payments 3.5% + Payments fees), Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, and Polar (all Merchant of Record, starting fee 5% + 50¢ per transaction). Mainland China is not on Stripe’s official supported country/region list (50 entries); Hong Kong is on the list. Whether each platform accepts individual registrations from mainland China follows each platform’s official supported-country/verification pages.

Payment monetization means an individual developer charging users abroad and settling the money. Two charging models: payment processing (the platform processes payments; tax and compliance responsibility stays with the seller) and Merchant of Record (the platform acts as the seller of record in a transaction, taking on tax, fraud protection, refund, and related responsibilities).

Group 1: Payment processing (1 entry)

Stripe is a payment processing platform; its Managed Payments product is a Merchant of Record.
