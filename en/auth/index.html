Authentication: 2 standalone auth free tiers and 2 database-included auth allowances

Updated 2026-11-14 · 2 standalone entries (verified 2026-11-13) · Specs and terms transcribed from vendor official pages

2 standalone auth services: Clerk Hobby (50,000 MRUs per app, 100 MROs per app, no credit card required) and Auth0 Free ($0, up to 25,000 MAU, sign-up needs no card). 2 database-included auth allowances: Supabase Auth (50,000 MAU) and Firebase Auth (50K MAU), each coming with its database free tier; full specs are on the database detail pages. MRU and MAU are different units of measurement; the numbers cannot be compared directly by size.

Authentication covers sign-up, sign-in, sessions, social logins, and organization management. Standalone auth services handle auth only; database-included auth allowances come with database free tiers, with full specs on the database detail pages.

Services outside the tables above, and why

The following services are outside the tables above, with the reason for each stated as a fact.
