Authentication: 8 standalone auth free tiers and 2 database-included auth allowances

Updated 2026-11-14 · 8 standalone entries (6 verified 2026-11-14, 2 verified 2026-11-13) · Specs and terms transcribed from vendor official pages

8 standalone auth services: Clerk Hobby (50,000 MRUs per app, 100 MROs per app, no credit card required), Auth0 Free ($0, up to 25,000 MAU, sign-up needs no card), Stytch (10,000 monthly active users and AI agents, unlimited organizations, 5 SSO/SCIM connections), Descope Free Forever (7,500 MAU, 10 tenants, 3 SSO connections), WorkOS AuthKit (up to 1,000,000 MAU), Logto Cloud Free (50,000 MAU, 50K tokens, no credit card required), Kinde Free (10,500 MAU, 5 organizations, no credit card required), and Frontegg (7,500 MAU, 5 enterprise connections, unlimited organizations). 2 database-included auth allowances: Supabase Auth (50,000 MAU) and Firebase Auth (50K MAU), each coming with its database free tier; full specs are on the database detail pages. MRU and MAU are different units of measurement; the numbers cannot be compared directly by size.

Authentication covers sign-up, sign-in, sessions, social logins, and organization management. Standalone auth services handle auth only; database-included auth allowances come with database free tiers, with full specs on the database detail pages.

Services outside the tables above, and why

The following services are outside the tables above, with the reason for each stated as a fact.

  • Neon auth (Managed Better Auth): the auth allowance comes with the Neon database free tier (60k MAU per project); see Neon free tier.
  • Clerk Pro through the GitHub Student Pack: a student-status offer, different from the Hobby free tier open to everyone.
  • PocketBase: self-hosted software, not a hosted auth service.
  • PropelAuth: the official pricing page text could not be read directly during verification, and second-hand accounts conflict (some state a 10,000 MAU free tier, others state no free plan); it stays outside the tables above.
  • AWS Cognito: the free allowance varies by account and user pool age and by tier (Lite / Essentials / Plus); not verified in this round, and outside the tables above.
  • Self-hosted software (Keycloak, SuperTokens, FusionAuth, and similar): self-hosted software, not hosted auth services.
