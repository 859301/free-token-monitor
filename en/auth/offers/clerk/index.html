MRU and MRO definitions

MRU (monthly retained user): a user counts as retained when returning 24 hours after signing up. Official wording: “A user is counted as retained when they return 24+ hours after signing up.” MRO (monthly retained organization): an organization has at least 2 members, with at least 1 being a retained user that month.

What the free tier includes

Official pricing page wording: “Free for your first 50,000 monthly retained users and 100 monthly retained orgs. No credit card required.” Per app: 50,000 MRUs and 100 MROs, unlimited apps, 3 dashboard seats, up to 3 social logins, fixed 7-day session, 1-day log retention, monthly creation and verification counts for API Keys and M2M Tokens, 5 user impersonations per month, custom domain included.

Overage rules and paid tiers

Exceeding 50,000 MRU requires upgrading to Pro; exceeding 100 MRO requires upgrading to the B2B add-on; the FAQ states a 1-month grace period. Pro $20/month (annual billing), including 50,000 MRUs, $0.02 each beyond that; Business $250/month (annual billing).
