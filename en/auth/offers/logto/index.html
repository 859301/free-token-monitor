MAU and token units

MAU: the maximum number of active users allowed in the Logto Cloud platform in a given period (month); the free tier allows up to 50K. Tokens: only access tokens are counted. “Unlimited” on the Pro tier means features without a fixed limit, subject to system policies for fair usage, security, and performance.

What the free tier includes

The free tier includes: up to 50,000 MAU, 50K tokens, 3 applications, 1 machine-to-machine app, 1 custom domain, 3 social connectors, 1 webhook, 3-day audit log retention, 1 tenant member; user authentication, password sign-in, passwordless email and SMS sign-in, email magic links, email and SMS connectors, user management, Account API, out-of-the-box account center, suspend users, invitation-only sign-up, security verification, password policy, signing key rotation, OIDC back-channel logout, CSRF and DoS protection, community chat support. The free tier excludes: passkey sign-in, organizations and organization roles, user roles, MFA, enterprise SSO, custom token claims, captcha and bot protection, and more.

What does the Logto free tier include?
