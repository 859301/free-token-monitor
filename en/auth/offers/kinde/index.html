MAU and MAO definitions

MAU: monthly active users; users on paid subscriptions are not counted toward MAU, and the FAQ states the same user is never charged twice. MAO (monthly active organizations): organizations containing a single active user are not counted. The free tier includes 5 MAO. The FAQ states Kinde charges only for active users; inactive and test users do not count toward billable usage.

What the free tier includes

Free includes: 10,500 MAU, 5 monthly active organizations, unlimited applications, unlimited dashboard seats; subscriptions and billing, email, SMS and social login, MFA, B2B management with organizations, a custom domain, internationalization, UI customization with HTML/CSS or React, workflows / actions, attack protection, machine-to-machine (M2M) applications; 5 custom roles and 10 permissions, 10 feature flags, 5 custom properties, 1 webhook, customizable session duration, 2 environments; 1 enterprise SSO connection, 2,000 M2M tokens, 10 SMS. The free tier excludes passkeys, API keys, custom OAuth2, and SCIM directory sync; Kinde branding cannot be removed (removal starts on Pro).

Overage rules and paid tiers

The FAQ states: “If you exceed your included limits, overages are billed automatically at transparent, published rates.” Free-tier overage rates do not appear in the table read — check the official page; Pro-tier rates are $0.0175 per extra MAU and $0.50 per extra MAO. The Pro feature list states Uncapped MAU while the pricing comparison table states extra usage at $0.0175 per MAU; both wordings are presented side by side — check the official page. Pro $25/month, Plus $75/month, Scale $250/month.
