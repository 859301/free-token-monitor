The MAU unit

The Auth0 free tier caps at up to 25,000 MAU, officially worded “External Active Users”. The pricing page FAQ confirms the 25,000 threshold. Details of how activity is counted are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. MAU and Clerk MRU are different units of measurement.

What the free tier includes

Free $0 includes: up to 25,000 MAU, 5 organizations, 1 enterprise connection, 1,000 M2M tokens, 5 Actions, 1-day log retention, 1 custom domain (card verification required). Pro MFA / role management are not included.

Custom domains and card verification

Sign-up needs no card; 1 custom domain requires card verification. Sign-up and custom domains are separate: card requirements for sign-up and card verification for custom domain activation are stated separately on the pricing page.

Paid tiers and overage

Essentials $35/month, Professional $240/month. Behavior past 25,000 MAU is not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.
