MAU and connection definitions

MAU: a user who logs in at least once in a calendar month; billing covers users who actually log in, not the total number of users in the account. Connections: SSO and SCIM are counted separately, so a customer can have SSO without SCIM or the other way around; a customer using both counts as two connections. The pay-as-you-go plan includes 5 in total, for example 5 SSO connections, or 3 SSO and 2 SCIM.

What the free tier includes

Always included: a fully customized hosted login, 7,500 MAU, 5 enterprise connections (SSO/SCIM), unlimited organizations, a custom domain. The FAQ states both plans include unlimited team seats and unlimited organizations (tenants). Enterprise adds: custom roles and permissions, entitlements and feature flags, per-tenant MFA, session and IP policies, advanced identity protection, multiple applications, hierarchies, unlimited environments, audit log streaming, private deployment options, a 99.99% uptime SLA, premium support with a dedicated Slack channel, and HIPAA BAAs, with custom pricing.

Overage rules and paid tiers

The pricing page is a usage-based estimator (sliders for MAU, enterprise connections, and M2M tokens); overage prices and behavior past 7,500 MAU or 5 enterprise connections are not stated in the official page text read — check the official page.
