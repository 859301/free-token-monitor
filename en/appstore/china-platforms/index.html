China-Specific App Stores and Mini Programs: Overview

Published 2026-11-16 · Updated 2026-11-16 · Verified 2026-11-15

Four China-specific platforms publish under rules documented on Chinese-language official pages: Alipay Mini Programs, Douyin Mini Programs, Huawei AppGallery, and Xiaomi App Store. Detail pages for these platforms are available in Chinese only, through the Chinese edition linked in the navigation. Rules change with the Chinese official pages, which govern in every case.

Alipay Mini Programs

Individual, sole-proprietor, and enterprise accounts can create mini programs: 5 per ID card for individuals (users aged 18 or older with mainland China identity information), 5 per sole proprietorship, and 10 per enterprise. Version review completes in 2 working days, under a dual review covering mini program admission and basic marketing norms. Alipay provides a free filing proxy channel, and newly launched mini programs must complete filing before launch. Registration fees are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. Detail page available in Chinese only: Alipay Mini Programs.

Douyin Mini Programs

The operating rules page covers submission, naming, content, function, and conduct rules: the certified entity must match the payment and advertising settlement entities, submissions must be complete and functional apps rather than test versions, embedded web pages must have completed filing, and restricted or paid features must come with a test account. Fees, eligibility, review times, and filing entry points are not stated on the page read — check the official page. Detail page available in Chinese only: Douyin Mini Programs operating rules.

Huawei AppGallery

Real-name certification covers individual and enterprise developers, with real-name review taking 1–2 working days; regular app review usually takes 24 hours, with expedited review available for urgent fixes. Certification details come from the Petal Ads direct-client account registration document, which shares its real-name system with the Developer Alliance. App names must match the name on the software copyright registration certificate. Registration fees and the full qualification checklist are not stated on the pages read — check the official page. Detail page available in Chinese only: Huawei AppGallery.

Xiaomi App Store

Developer accounts cover individual and enterprise types, with identity review in 1–3 working days and app review in 1–3 working days. Apps must complete filing before launch; a software copyright certificate of one of two accepted types is required, and the app main title must match the certificate name. Registration fees are not stated on the pages read — check the official page. Material details on the older submission tutorial pages (2013–2014 screenshots) are stale; current HyperOS documentation governs. Detail page available in Chinese only: Xiaomi App Store.

MIIT app filing

Under MIIT notice Xin Guan [2023] No. 105, organizers of apps providing internet information services inside China must complete filing; apps without filing must not provide app internet information services. Filing runs through a network access provider or app distribution platform via the national internet basic resources management system, addressed to the communications administration of the organizer domicile; complete and accurate materials receive filing within twenty working days, with a filing number issued. Filing fees, the materials checklist, and SMS verification details are not covered in the notice text — check the official page. The full account, in Chinese, sits in the app filing guide of the Chinese edition.

WeChat Mini Programs, OPPO Software Store, vivo App Store, and Tencent App Store: official page information could not be confirmed for fees, eligibility, review times, or qualification requirements — check the official page.
