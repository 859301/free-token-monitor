Fees

The Apple Developer Program costs 99 USD per membership year. Prices may vary by region and are shown in local currency during enrollment. The pages read do not state a China mainland CNY price; no conversion applies — check the official page. The Apple Developer Enterprise Program annual fee is 299 USD, with prices varying by region; the Enterprise Program requires separate identity verification and web enrollment for the organization. Whether individual and organization fees are identical is not stated separately on the pages read; a single 99 USD price line covers membership as a whole — check the official page.

Fee waivers

Nonprofit organizations, accredited educational institutions, and government entities that meet the requirements can apply for a fee waiver. Four conditions apply: status as a legal entity that is a nonprofit organization, accredited educational institution, or government entity; not an individual, sole proprietor, or single-person business; no signed Paid Applications Agreement for paid apps or in-app purchases (or termination of that agreement); and no sale of digital goods or services through any app by other means. An approved waiver removes the annual membership fee; membership fees already paid are not refunded, in full or in part. Waiver eligibility must be confirmed by the account holder at each annual renewal; renewal opens 30 days before expiry. After a waiver is revoked, the 99 USD annual membership fee (or the local-currency equivalent) applies.

Individual and organization enrollment

Enrollment covers individuals and organizations. An individual or sole proprietor needs an Apple Account with two-factor authentication turned on and must meet the legal age in the region; enrollment uses the legal name, and the seller name field shows the individual legal name. For an organization, the person enrolling must hold legal authority to bind the organization; the organization must be a legal entity able to enter contracts with Apple, with no acceptance of DBAs, fictitious business names, trade names, or branches; the organization name shows as the seller name; the organization (except a government entity) must hold a D-U-N-S number, a nine-digit number assigned by Dun & Bradstreet; the work email must be associated with the organization domain; and the organization website must be publicly available, functional, and associated with the organization domain.

Review times and common issues

App Review works around the clock, typically reviewing at least 50% of submissions in less than 24 hours and 90% in less than 48 hours. An incomplete submission can delay review or fail review. On average, more than 40% of unresolved issues relate to guideline 2.1 App Completeness, covering crashes, placeholder content, and incomplete information. Common issues on the official page include: crashes and bugs, broken links, placeholder content, incomplete information (demo account and special configuration notes required), privacy policy issues, unclear data-access descriptions, inaccurate screenshots, interface quality shortfalls, web clippings, content aggregators, and link collections (guideline 4.2), repeated submission of similar apps (guideline 4.3), copycats, misleading users, insufficient lasting value, and incorrect submitter (certain apps in highly regulated fields such as banking and finance, cryptocurrency, health, gambling, and air travel must come from the legal entity providing the service, not an individual developer).

Appeals and expedited review

A rejected submission can be appealed to the App Review Board, once per rejected submission. Expedited review is available for special circumstances, such as a critical bug fix or an event launch. A 30-minute video consultation can be booked to discuss the review guidelines and common rejection reasons.

Commissions: Small Business Program

The App Store Small Business Program carries a reduced commission rate of 15% on paid apps and Apple In-App Purchases. Existing developers who made up to 1 million USD in proceeds in the prior calendar year for all apps, as well as developers new to the App Store, can qualify for the program and the reduced commission. A participating developer who surpasses the 1 million USD threshold in the current calendar year pays the standard commission rate on future sales. Proceeds are adjusted fifteen (15) days after the end of the fiscal calendar month in which enrollment is approved. For developers on the alternative terms in the EU in the App Store Small Business Program and for subscriptions after the first year, Apple offers a further reduced commission of 10%. The page read states only “standard commission rate” without a figure — check the official page; the general post-first-year subscription rate is not covered on the page read — check the official page.

TestFlight

External testers number up to 10,000 people and internal testers up to 100 App Store Connect users with access to the content. For external testing, a beta build may require review: the first build added to a group goes to App Review for confirmation against the App Review Guidelines, only the first build requires review, and later builds may not need a full review; testing starts after the build is approved. A build becomes unavailable for testers after 90 days. Testers install the free TestFlight app and accept an invitation by email or public link. The TestFlight overview page read does not price TestFlight separately — check the official page.

China mainland requirements

Chinese law requires some apps to provide additional documentation for distribution on the China mainland App Store; the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) requires some apps to hold a valid Internet Content Provider (ICP) filing number; the ICP filing number and metadata on the App Information page must match the information filed with MIIT; a verified and saved ICP filing number appears on the App Store product page. The official page gives no list of which apps fall under “some apps” — check the official page. Games require an edition number, book and magazine content apps require an internet publishing license from the National Press and Publication Administration, religious content apps require an internet religious information license from the National Religious Affairs Administration, and news content apps require an internet news information license from the Cyberspace Administration of China. Application details are not stated on the page read — check the official page.

Questions

Can an individual developer enroll in the Apple Developer Program?

Yes. An individual or sole proprietor needs an Apple Account with two-factor authentication turned on and must meet the legal age in the region; enrollment uses the legal name, and the seller name field shows the individual legal name.

Who can apply for a fee waiver?

Nonprofit organizations, accredited educational institutions, and government entities that meet four conditions: status as a legal entity of the qualifying type; not an individual, sole proprietor, or single-person business; no signed Paid Applications Agreement; and no sale of digital goods or services through any app. An approved waiver removes the annual fee; fees already paid are not refunded.

How long does a TestFlight build stay available?

A build becomes unavailable for testers after 90 days. External testers number up to 10,000 people and internal testers up to 100 App Store Connect users with access to the content; only the first build added to a group requires review.
