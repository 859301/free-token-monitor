Free scope and paywall boundary

The full license page mentions no paid tier — check the official page. The license page gives no illustration count — not verified. The license page states no geographic restriction.

Prohibited uses

  • The license does not cover compiling unDraw assets to replicate a similar or competing service (in any form), or distributing assets as a package or otherwise.
  • The ban extends to linking, embedding, scraping, searching, or downloading site assets without consent, by automated or non-automated means.
  • Expressly prohibited: use of assets to train, fine-tune, or develop artificial intelligence, machine learning models, or similar technology, including use as generative AI training data, inclusion in ML datasets, fine-tuning models, training/validating/testing AI systems, and automated processing of assets to develop AI/ML models. Such use needs separate express written permission from unDraw.

Brand logos in illustrations

Brand logos included in illustrations are registered trademarks of their respective owners, included for promotional purposes, with no association with unDraw or the user and no endorsement by trademark holders of unDraw or vice versa; provided only to present the brand, service, or company itself, with no other use allowed.

FAQ

Can unDraw illustrations be used commercially?

Yes. The license page grants free use of all images, assets, and vector graphics for commercial and non-commercial purposes, with modification allowed and no attribution required.

Can unDraw illustrations be bundled into a paid asset pack?

No. The license does not cover distributing assets as a package or otherwise, or compiling assets to replicate a similar or competing service.

Can unDraw illustrations train an AI model?

No. The license page expressly prohibits use for training, fine-tuning, or developing AI or ML models or similar technology; such use needs separate express written permission from unDraw.
