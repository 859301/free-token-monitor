Free scope and paywall boundary

The terms and help page mention no paid tier or download limit — check the official page. The free scope and rate limits of the Pexels API are not verified — check the official page.

Use cases named on the license page include websites, e-commerce, newsletters, ebooks, presentations, blogs, templates for sale, ads, banners, marketing campaigns, printed items (flyers, postcards, magazines, books, CD covers), and social media. The terms state no geographic restriction.

Prohibited uses

  • No standalone sale or distribution of content, in digital or physical form: images, audio, videos, NFTs, other digital files (including through stock platforms), printed items, wallpapers, posters, merchandise, or physical products.
  • Standalone means no creative effort applied and essentially the same form as on the platform; adding only a filter, color change, resize, or crop still counts as standalone; combining the content with other assets into a new creative work does not.
  • No use of content as a trademark, design mark, trade name, business name, or service mark, or as part of one.
  • Content depicting trademarks, logos, or brands must not be used for commercial purposes for goods and services, especially printed on products for sale or other physical products.
  • No unethical or unlawful use of content (especially involving identifiable people), including pornographic, obscene, offensive, unlawful, defamatory, hateful, or threatening content and political contexts.
  • No misleading or deceptive use, including implying that a depicted person or brand endorses the user, or implying that the content was created by the user.

The terms state: some content may be subject to other intellectual property, moral rights, property rights, privacy rights, and similar constraints (identifiable people, logos, brands, products, buildings, audio/video samples, organizations); before commercial use, check whether third-party consent or a license is needed — responsibility rests with the user; Pexels does not guarantee that any consent or license has been obtained and expressly disclaims related liability.

FAQ

Can Pexels photos and videos be used commercially?

Yes. The Pexels License grants download, copy, modify, and use rights for commercial or non-commercial purposes, with no attribution required. Rights in depicted people and trademarks are not covered; check whether third-party consent or a license is needed before commercial use.

Can a Pexels photo be printed on merchandise and sold as-is?

No. Standalone sale and distribution are prohibited, including printing on products for sale or other physical products; adding only a filter, color change, resize, or crop still counts as standalone. Combining the content with other assets into a new creative work does not count as standalone.

What needs attention when a photo shows a person or a brand logo?

Depicted rights are not covered: identifiable people involve portrait and privacy rights, and content depicting trademarks must not be used for commercial purposes for goods and services. Pexels does not guarantee that any consent or license has been obtained; responsibility rests with the user.
