Font Awesome Free (icons)

Published 2026-11-15 · Updated 2026-11-15 · Source: Font Awesome official free license page · Verified 2026-11-14

IconsCC BY 4.0OFL 1.1MITCommercial useEmbedded notice

Font Awesome Free combines three licenses: icons CC BY 4.0, fonts SIL OFL 1.1, code MIT; commercial projects are allowed; all three licenses require attribution, but downloaded files already contain sufficient embedded attribution comments, so normal use needs nothing additional. The Free plan is $0/year with 1 Project Kit and 10k pageviews a month. Verified 2026-11-14; check the official page.

Prohibited uses

  • No use of the Font Awesome name in a project name.
  • All brand icons are trademarks of their respective owners; use does not imply endorsement by trademark holders of Font Awesome or vice versa; do not use brand logos for any purpose other than referring to the company, product, or service represented.

Notice retention and attribution

All three licenses require attribution; downloaded Free files already contain sufficient embedded attribution comments, so normal use needs nothing additional (source text: you shouldn't need to do anything additional). The license page asks users not to remove comments, especially in code. Whether visible on-screen attribution is required is not required by the license page — check the license page.

FAQ

Does the free version need on-screen attribution?

The license page does not require visible on-screen attribution. Downloaded Free files already contain sufficient embedded attribution comments, so normal use needs nothing additional; the license page asks users not to remove comments, especially in code.

How does the free plan differ from paid plans?

The Free plan is $0/year with 1 Project Kit and 10k pageviews a month, excluding Pro icons, Pro+ icons, icon downloads, hosted npm, API access to Pro icons, perpetual licenses, and Figma. Paid plans are Starter $48/year, Solo $96/year, Team $192/year (annual prices); Pro+ icons are included only in Solo and Team.

Can brand icons be used as a logo?

No. Brand icons are trademarks of their respective owners and serve only to refer to the company, product, or service represented, with no other purpose allowed.
