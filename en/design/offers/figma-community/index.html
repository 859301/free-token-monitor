Free files and templates

Under CC BY 4.0, free files can be shared and adapted for any purpose, including commercial purposes: sharing means copying and redistributing in any medium or format, and adapting means transforming and building upon the material; community members must give credit to the original author. Creators can choose to add other license types on top.

Paid resources

A buyer of a paid file can share file contents with up to five editors and incorporate file contents into an unlimited number of outputs (designs, apps, websites, and similar), but outputs must differ significantly from the file contents, must involve time, effort, and skill, and their primary value must not come from the file contents themselves; files must not be resold or redistributed (beyond the allowed scope). Paid-file creators cannot offer commercial files under other terms; plugin and widget creators can supplement the license. Free preview content inside paid Figma Design files uses CC BY 4.0.

FAQ

Can free Figma Community files be used commercially?

Yes. Free files default to CC BY 4.0, shareable and adaptable for any purpose including commercial purposes, with credit to the original author; expressly not a public domain license; creators can add other license types.

Do plugins share the same license as files?

No. Free plugins and widgets default to the Community Free Resource License, runnable in files for any purpose, with no resale or redistribution; creators can supplement or replace the terms.

Can a purchased paid file be resold?

Files must not be resold or redistributed beyond the allowed scope. A buyer can share contents with up to five editors and incorporate contents into an unlimited number of outputs, but outputs must differ significantly and their primary value must not come from the file contents themselves.
