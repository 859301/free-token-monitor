Help desk systems compared: Zoho Desk, Help Scout, Jira Service Management, Freshdesk

Published 2026-11-15 · Updated 2026-11-15 · Verified 2026-11-14

All four use ticketing and shared inbox forms, with differences in duration, seats, and cap metering: Zoho Desk covers 3 user licenses with email ticketing as the core; Help Scout covers up to 5 users, 100 contacts a month, and 10 articles; Jira Service Management covers 3 agents and 2GB, with customers free and unlimited; Freshdesk is a 6-month time-limited free plan. Verified 2026-11-14.

Duration note: The Freshdesk Free Program runs 6 months free; upgrading to a paid tier is required at expiry or past 2 agents. Not a permanent free tier.

Duration first

The Freshdesk Free Program runs 6 months free, with an upgrade required at expiry; the Zoho Desk trial downgrades automatically to the Free Edition after 15 days; Jira Service Management states free forever; Help Scout permanent wording is not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.

Cap metering

Help Scout counts contacts (100 a month, with multiple conversations from one person counting as one contact), plus a 10-article cap; Jira Service Management counts agents and storage (3 agents, 2GB); Zoho Desk counts user licenses (3); Freshdesk counts agents and duration (2 agents, 6 months). Metering differs, so direct ranking across units does not apply.

Ticketing and knowledge base placement

Zoho Desk Free Edition centers on email ticketing; Help Scout Free tier includes 1 Docs site and 10 articles; Jira Service Management and Freshdesk feature coverage — check the official page.

How to choose

With email ticketing as the core and 3 people or fewer, Zoho Desk fits; with help articles needed and 100 contacts a month or fewer, Help Scout fits; with ITSM capabilities needed and many customers, Jira Service Management fits; with a 6-month horizon accepted, Freshdesk fits, with paid tier prices confirmed before expiry.

The Help Scout Free tier includes 1 Docs site and 10 articles; Zoho Desk Free Edition knowledge base coverage — check the official page; Jira Service Management and Freshdesk — check the official page.

No. Upgrading to a paid tier is required at expiry; more than 2 agents also requires an upgrade.

Help Scout free accounts need no payment method; the Zoho Desk trial needs no credit card and the Free Edition carries no billing; other card requirements — check the official page.
