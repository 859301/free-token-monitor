Polar tiers compared

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: Polar’s four tiers: Starter free at 5% + 50¢ per transaction with standard support; Pro $20/month at 3.8% + 40¢ with priority support, official break-even point about $1,379/month in sales; Growth $100/month at 3.6% + 35¢ with priority support, about $5,634/month; Scale $400/month at 3.4% + 30¢ with Slack + priority support, about $19,048/month. International cards add 1.5%, and disputes cost $15 each.

How to read the break-even points

Break-even points are official page figures: at that monthly sales figure, the fee savings of a paid tier roughly offset the monthly fee. Below the break-even point, Starter has the lower total cost; above it, the paid tier has the lower total cost. Break-even figures are quoted from the official page; actual costs vary with transaction count and per-transaction amounts — check the official page.

Early Member rule

Organizations created before 2026-05-27 permanently keep the 4% + 40¢ + subscription fee +0.5% rate. Upgrading to a paid plan permanently loses that rate; dropping back to Starter uses the new 5% + 50¢ rate. Current tier status: check the official page.

How to choose

Match monthly sales against the break-even points: below about $1,379/month, Starter has the lower total cost; above about $1,379/month, Pro starts to cost less; above about $5,634/month, compare Growth; above about $19,048/month, compare Scale. On support, Pro and Growth include priority support, and Scale adds Slack support. Break-even figures are official page figures; see Polar fees.

FAQ

When does Polar Pro cost less than Starter?

The official break-even point is about $1,379/month in sales. Above that figure, Pro has the lower total cost. Verified 2026-11-13.

Can Early Member status return to the old rate after upgrading?

No. Upgrading to a paid plan permanently loses that rate, and dropping back to Starter uses the new 5% + 50¢ rate. Verified 2026-11-13.
