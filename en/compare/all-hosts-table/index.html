FAQ

Which of the 10 hosts has the most space?

FreeHosting.com, 10 GB, but an owned domain is required and SSL needs a paid add-on. Next is the 5 GB tier: InfinityFree, Byet.host (NVMe), ProFreeHost, and TinkerHost (5 GiB). Verified 2026-11-13.

Which of the 10 hosts clearly need no credit card?

7 state no credit card on the official site: InfinityFree, Byet.host, x10Hosting, GoogieHost, FreeHosting.com, ProFreeHost, TinkerHost. Card requirements for AwardSpace, HelioHost, and FreeHostia are not stated on the official pages read. Verified 2026-11-13.
