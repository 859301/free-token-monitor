Per-message and per-segment metering differ

Twilio bills per segment on the FAQ wording (the overview says per message); Telnyx and Bird bill per segment; Vonage, Plivo, Sinch, Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, and GCP bill per message; Textbelt bills per tier bundle. Long messages bill per split segment, with segment counts depending on character set and length; see the FAQ. Cross-metering rankings mislead: a segment and a message are not the same unit for long messages.

Carrier surcharges are billed separately

Twilio, Sinch, Telnyx, and Bird prices all add carrier fees on top; Plivo publishes per-carrier surcharge details. Surcharge amounts vary by carrier and number type and belong in total cost comparisons.

Number and 10DLC fees

Number rental and 10DLC registration are fixed costs beyond per-message prices: Twilio 10DLC brand registration $4.50 or $46 one-time, with campaign review and monthly fees on top; Sinch Engage 10DLC $25/month; Plivo short code rental from $500 per month. The Bird 10DLC fees section carries no amounts — check the official page.

Packages vs pay-as-you-go

Sinch Engage is plan-based ($99/month with 3,000 messages and up), Alibaba Cloud and Tencent Cloud use package bundles (Alibaba Cloud in-package per-message prices below pay-as-you-go rates), and the rest are mostly pay-as-you-go. Package validity and coverage appear on each detail page.

FAQ

Which US SMS price is cheapest?

Metering differs, so no direct ranking applies: Bird from $0.0035 per segment and Telnyx from $0.004 per part bill per segment, while Plivo at $0.0077 per message bills per message, all plus carrier fees.

Do prices include carrier surcharges?

Most bill surcharges separately: Twilio, Sinch, Telnyx, and Bird prices add carrier fees on top, and Plivo publishes per-carrier details.

How are long messages billed?

Long messages bill per split segment, with segment counts depending on character set and length; GSM pure English splits at 160/153, Chinese at 70/67 — see the FAQ.
