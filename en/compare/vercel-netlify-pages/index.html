Metering differs

Vercel meters by separate allowances: transfer, requests, function invocations, and CPU hours each have a number. Netlify meters by credits: 300 credits per month, with deploys, compute, bandwidth, and requests all consuming credits. Pages limits by builds and files: build count, file count, and single-file size have explicit numbers, while whether requests and bandwidth are free and unlimited does not appear on the limits documentation page.

Build comparison

Vercel: Basic machines, deployment storage 10 GB. Netlify: 1 concurrent build, production deploys 15 credits each. Pages: 1 concurrent build, 500 builds/month, 20-minute build timeout, unlimited preview deployments.

Commercial limits

Of the three, only Vercel states in the Terms of Service that the Hobby plan is limited to personal or non-commercial use (§4), with a two-column Fair Use list. Commercial-use limits for Netlify and Pages are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.

How to choose

Choose by metering: for separate allowance numbers, see Vercel’s transfer, request, function invocation, and CPU hour figures; for a credits model, see Netlify’s 300 credits and cost table; for explicit build and file numbers, see Pages’ 500 builds/month and 20,000 files. For commercial use: Vercel Hobby is limited to personal or non-commercial use, while commercial-use limits for Netlify and Pages follow the official page. For stated overage consequences: Vercel states no additional purchases and a 30-day waiting period, while behavior after exceeding the cap is not stated on the official pages read for Netlify and Pages.
