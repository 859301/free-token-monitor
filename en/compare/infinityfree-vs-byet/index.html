InfinityFree vs Byet.host

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: both hosts are 5 GB, ad-free, and need no credit card; the differences are database counts (400 vs unlimited), PHP versions (8.4 vs 8.3), the SSL flow, the panel, limit transparency, and idle policies: the InfinityFree hits cap (50,000/day) and idle rule (5 hits in 30 days) both have explicit numbers; the Byet.host TOS gives no hits number or idle day count, but states a cap of 3 accounts per person and a $9.99 backup-creation fee.

Limit transparency

The InfinityFree hits cap is an explicit number: 50,000/day, offline for 24 hours over the cap, then automatic recovery, with the free-tier quota not raisable on request. The full Byet.host TOS gives no hits/day number; the official free page states Unlimited bandwidth and “No hidden traffic caps”, while the TOS reserves the right to suspend excessive resource use without notice. The two hosts’ hits figures cannot be swapped.

Idle policies

InfinityFree: fewer than 5 hits in 30 days leads to suspension, reactivatable in the client area; suspended accounts are deleted automatically after 20–60 days (another KB passage writes only “some time”, with no day count). Byet.host: the TOS writes only about reserving the right to set an idle retention day count, to log out long-idle accounts, and to terminate long-idle accounts without notice, with no specific day count.

What is the relationship between the two hosts

Belonging to the same iFastNet ecosystem is a second-hand claim, unconfirmed by direct official-page reads. Supporting evidence: the Byet TOS privacy policy link points to ifastnet.com. The ProFreeHost official homepage is marked Powered by iFastNet.com.

How to choose

For explicit cap and idle numbers: InfinityFree, with explicit hits cap and idle figures. For unlimited databases and VistaPanel: Byet.host. For zero-configuration SSL on an owned domain: the InfinityFree free SSL certificate; Byet.host owned-domain SSL needs a manual panel install. For more than 3 free accounts: Byet.host caps accounts at 3 per person, while the InfinityFree account cap is not stated on the official pages read.
