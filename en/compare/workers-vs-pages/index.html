Metering differs

Workers is metered by requests and CPU: 100,000 requests/day, 10 ms CPU per invocation, no duration charge. Pages is limited by builds and files: 500 builds/month, 20,000 files/site, single file 25 MiB. The two free amounts use different units and cannot be compared directly by size.

Workers companion free amounts

KV Free: 100,000 reads/day, 1,000 writes/day, 1 GB storage. D1 Free: 5M row reads/day, 100,000 row writes/day, 5 GB total storage, 10 databases. Queues Free: 10,000 ops/day. Workers Logs Free: 200,000/day, 3-day retention.

The claim about new projects using Workers

A claim that Pages documentation recommends Workers for new projects was not confirmed on the Pages limits documentation page — check the official page. The Pages limits documentation was updated on 2026-09-05.

How to choose

Choose by metering: for request and CPU metering, see Workers’ 100,000 requests/day and 10 ms CPU; for build and file limits, see Pages’ 500 builds/month and 20,000 files. For KV, D1, and Queues free amounts, see the companion figures on the Workers pricing page.
