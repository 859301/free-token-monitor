Verify billing compared: per-success fees vs channel-only billing

Published 2026-11-16 · Updated 2026-11-16 · Verified 2026-11-15

Verify billing follows two structures: a per-success verification platform fee plus channel fees (Twilio, Vonage), and zero verification fees or no platform fee, with billing per channel and destination only (Plivo, Bird). Platform fees and channel fees are counted separately

Twilio and Vonage verification fees cover only the verification platform, with SMS channel fees billed separately: Twilio US SMS at $0.0083 per SMS, Vonage with message and voice rates on top. Plivo and Bird charge no verification platform fee, so all costs sit in channel fees: Plivo charges SMS, voice, or WhatsApp channel charges only, and Bird bills per channel and destination with carrier fees on top. Total cost comparisons must combine platform and channel fees rather than reading either one alone.

Failed attempts

Only Twilio states a failed-attempt rule on the pages read: failed, expired, or incomplete verifications skip the $0.05 base fee, but SMS attempts are always billed. Failed-attempt billing for other services is on the official page.

Telnyx wording shown side by side

The Telnyx Verify pricing page header says From $0.05 a verification, with delivery cost inside the rate; the rate table says $0.03 per successful verification plus SMS API pricing. The two wordings differ and are shown side by side, with no ruling — check the official page.

How to choose

With small verification volumes and concentrated destinations, channel fees and surcharges decide the comparison; with large volumes, Twilio custom pricing is usually evaluated above 100,000 verifications per month, and Vonage and Telnyx platform-fee wording needs confirmation on the official page. For SMS codes without a verification platform, the Plivo and Bird channel-fee structures are simpler; for platform-hosted templates, GCP Identity Platform per-message billing with the first 10 SMS per day not billed is the reference.

FAQ

Why does Verify bill twice?

The platform fee covers the verification service itself and the channel fee covers SMS delivery, billed separately; Plivo and Bird charge no verification platform fee, only channel fees.

Which services charge no verification platform fee?

Plivo Verify charges zero verification fees and Bird Verify carries no plan, seat, or platform fee; GCP Identity Platform states no separate verification platform fee and bills per SMS sent.

Are failed verifications billed?

Twilio skips the base fee for failed or expired attempts but always bills SMS attempts; other services are on the official page.
