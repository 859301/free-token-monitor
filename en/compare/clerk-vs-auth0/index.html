Clerk vs Auth0

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: the units differ: Clerk counts MRU (50,000 MRUs per app; a user counts as retained when returning 24 hours after signing up), Auth0 counts MAU (up to 25,000 MAU, officially worded External Active Users). The two numbers cannot be compared directly by size. Social logins: Clerk up to 3; Auth0 has 1 enterprise connection. Organizations: Clerk 100 MROs per app, Auth0 5 organizations. Cards: Clerk needs no credit card; Auth0 sign-up needs no card, custom domains require card verification. Log retention is 1 day for both. Overage: Clerk states required upgrades and a 1-month grace period; Auth0 overage behavior is not stated on the official pages read.

Units differ

Clerk MRU definition: a user counts as retained when returning 24 hours after signing up. Official wording: “A user is counted as retained when they return 24+ hours after signing up.” Auth0 MAU is worded “External Active Users”; details of how activity is counted are not stated on the official pages read. The units differ, so 50,000 and 25,000 cannot be compared directly by size.

Social logins and enterprise connections

Clerk Hobby allows up to 3 social logins; Auth0 Free has 1 enterprise connection. Social logins and enterprise connections serve different sign-in scenarios.

Organizations

Clerk counts organizations in MRO: an organization has at least 2 members, with at least 1 being a retained user that month; Hobby allows 100 MROs per app. Auth0 Free has 5 organizations. The two units differ, so the numbers cannot be compared directly by size.

Cards and custom domains

Clerk states “No credit card required” on the official site, with a custom domain included. Auth0 sign-up needs no card; 1 custom domain requires card verification. Card requirements for Clerk custom domains are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.

Overage rules

Clerk states overage rules: exceeding 50,000 MRU requires upgrading to Pro, exceeding 100 MRO requires upgrading to the B2B add-on, with a 1-month grace period stated in the FAQ. Auth0 behavior past 25,000 MAU is not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.

How to choose

By unit: Clerk counts retained users, Auth0 counts external active users; the definition wording is above. By social logins and enterprise connections: Clerk up to 3 social logins, Auth0 1 enterprise connection. By organizations: Clerk 100 MROs per app, Auth0 5 organizations. By card: neither needs a card for sign-up; Auth0 custom domains require card verification. By overage: Clerk states required upgrades and a grace period; Auth0 overage behavior follows the official page.
