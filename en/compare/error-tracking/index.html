Period wording differs

Sentry’s 5k errors, 5M spans, and 50 replays state no period in the three rows; PostHog’s 100K exceptions, 5K recordings, and 10 GB logs are monthly free allowances. The two cannot be compared directly on the same period.

Users and projects

The user/project trade-off runs opposite: Sentry limits 1 user with unlimited projects; PostHog has unlimited team members with only 1 project. Retention differs too: Sentry 30-day lookback, PostHog 1-year retention.

How to choose

Three points: error-volume wording (no period stated vs monthly), users and projects (1 user with unlimited projects vs unlimited members with 1 project), overage behavior (not stated vs stops at the limit).
