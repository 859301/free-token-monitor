Resend vs SendGrid vs Mailgun

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: the biggest difference among the three is duration: Resend is a free tier (FAQ states free-forever), Mailgun is not stated as permanent on the official site, and SendGrid has only a 60-day trial. The daily cap is 100 emails/day for all three, but Resend also has a 3,000 emails/month monthly cap, while Mailgun and the SendGrid trial are metered daily.

Duration: free tier, not stated as permanent, trial

The Resend FAQ states a free-forever plan is offered; the Mailgun pricing page contains no "forever" wording, and duration is not stated as permanent; the SendGrid pricing page has no permanent free plan, only a 60-day trial, and the old permanent free plan was retired in March–May 2025 (multiple second-hand sources agree).

Limit metering

All three show a 100 emails/day daily cap: Resend also has a 3,000 emails/month monthly cap; Mailgun and the SendGrid trial are metered daily. Resend has one more counting rule: received emails count toward the transactional quota, while Broadcast emails do not count toward the transactional limit. For limit structures, see Daily vs monthly limits.

Overage behavior

Resend: pay-as-you-go is only available on paid plans; without it, an overage triggers a notification, with an option to upgrade. SendGrid: overage may be charged. Mailgun free plan overage behavior is not stated on the official pages read — check the official page.

Paid starting prices

Resend Pro $20/month for 50,000 emails, Scale $90/month for 100,000 emails, overage $0.90/1,000 emails. SendGrid Essentials from $19.95/month, Pro from $89.95/month, Premier custom pricing; tier prices and overage rates in the official pricing PDF are on the SendGrid trial page. Mailgun Basic from $15/month for 10,000 emails/month, Foundation $35/month for 50,000 emails (first month free), Scale $90/month for 100,000 emails (first month free).

FAQ

Which of the three is long-term free?

The Resend FAQ states a free-forever plan is offered; Mailgun is not stated as permanent on the official site; SendGrid has only a 60-day trial and no permanent free plan. Verified 2026-11-13.

All three allow 100 emails a day — what is the difference?

Resend also has a 3,000 emails/month monthly cap on top of 100 emails/day; Mailgun and the SendGrid trial are metered daily; the SendGrid trial expires after 60 days. Verified 2026-11-13.
