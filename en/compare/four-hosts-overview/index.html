“Unlimited bandwidth” and the hits cap

“Unlimited bandwidth” does not mean unlimited visits. InfinityFree writes Unlimited bandwidth, while the official KB writes a 50,000 hits/day cap: a hit is every request for a website file, and a page with 2 CSS + 3 JS + 5 images produces 11 hits on one load; at the cap, the account goes offline for 24 hours. Byet.host writes Unlimited bandwidth and “No hidden traffic caps”, while the TOS gives no hits/day number and reserves the right to suspend excessive resource use without notice. The x10Hosting official homepage states no bandwidth figure. AwardSpace traffic is an explicit number: 5 GB per month, about 5,000 visits/month. Judging whether traffic is enough means reading the bandwidth wording together with the hits/visitor cap.

How to choose

For large space and many databases: InfinityFree (5 GB, 400 databases) and Byet.host (5 GB NVMe, unlimited databases) have the largest figures. For explicit cap numbers: the InfinityFree hits cap and idle rules both have explicit numbers; the AwardSpace traffic and database limits are also explicit numbers (1 GB, 5 GB/month, 1 database of 30 MB). For a DirectAdmin panel: x10Hosting. For small space needs with a 30-day login accepted: x10Hosting, with 500 MB starting space, 2 databases, 3 domains. For free SSL with zero configuration: the InfinityFree free SSL certificate and the Byet.host automatic subdomain SSL; x10Hosting and AwardSpace SSL are not stated on the official pages read.
