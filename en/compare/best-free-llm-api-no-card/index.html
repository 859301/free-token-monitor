How to choose

  • Long-term free, no claiming: Zhipu GLM Flash — officially marked fully free, callable right after registering; only the Flash series is free, flagship models are paid.
  • Most models through one key: OpenRouter :free models — 20 RPM / 50 requests per day, rising to 1,000 per day after the account has been topped up $10. Note that failed requests also count toward the daily cap.
  • Speed: Groq Free Plan — per-model limits of 30 RPM / 1,000 requests per day / 200K tokens per day; limits are calculated per organization and per model.
  • Long context: Gemini API free tier — Flash / Flash-Lite and Gemini 4 Hydrogen models with context up to 1M; limits are 5–30 RPM with per-model daily caps, and actual limits are shown in the AI Studio quota panel.
  • Simplest daily allowance: Cloudflare Workers AI — 10,000 Neurons per day, resetting at 00:00 UTC (08:00 Beijing time); some newer models require a payment method first.
  • Credit-style balance: SiliconFlow — ¥14 credit on signup, plus a ¥16 voucher (180 days) for invite + real-name verification; real-name verification is required.

All six are API access and OpenAI-compatible. None is unlimited: each has rate limits, a daily cap, or a credit balance. For the full picture of how free allowances differ, see the free-tier types comparison.
