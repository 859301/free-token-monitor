SendGrid trial vs free tiers

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: SendGrid has only a 60-day trial and no permanent free plan; Resend, Brevo, and Postmark are free tiers. After the 60-day trial expires, continued use requires a paid plan or a move to another service, while free tiers carry no trial-period wording. The permanent free plan recommended in old tutorials was retired in March–May 2025.

The duration difference

A trial has a fixed expiry date: the SendGrid trial runs 60 days, and the plan header reads "Free Trial $0/mo for 60 days". Free tiers carry no trial-period wording: the Resend FAQ states a free-forever plan is offered, the Brevo official help center states no time limit, and the Postmark pricing page states "never expires or runs out". Mailgun differs: permanence is not stated on the official site — see Mailgun free plan.

After the trial expires

After the 60-day SendGrid trial expires, continued use requires moving to a paid plan (Essentials from $19.95/month, Pro from $89.95/month, Premier custom pricing) or moving to another service. Account handling details after trial expiry — check the official page.

Old free plan retired

The old SendGrid permanent free plan was retired in March–May 2025 (multiple second-hand sources agree): on 2025-03-25 new accounts moved to the trial, and on 2025-05-27 the retirement of the existing free plan after 60 days was announced. The "SendGrid free plan" wording in old tutorials no longer holds against the current pricing page.
