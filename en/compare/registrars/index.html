First-year prices and renewal prices

First-year prices: Spaceship $9.48 (promo price) is the lowest, Porkbun $11.81 next, Namecheap $12.08 (promo price) the highest; Namecheap also has a new-customer-only first-year promo of $6.48/year. Renewal prices: Spaceship $10.68 is the lowest, Porkbun $11.81 next, Namecheap $19.98 the highest. Porkbun charges the same price for registration, renewal, and transfer, with no gap between a first-year promo price and the renewal price; Namecheap and Spaceship first-year prices are both promo prices, with renewal charged at each renewal price.

Three ICANN fee wordings

Porkbun: prices include ICANN and all other fees (All prices include ICANN and any other fees). Namecheap: $0.20 per purchase, added to the list price at purchase, applies to some domains. Spaceship: $0.20/year, charged mandatorily at purchase (official FAQ wording: ICANN charges a mandatory annual fee of $0.20 at the time of purchase).

2026-11-01 wholesale price increase

From 2026-11-01, the Verisign .com wholesale price rose from $10.26 to $10.97; with the $0.20 ICANN fee, the .com cost floor is $11.17. Price readings from before 2026-11-01 are outdated: the old Porkbun price $11.08, the old Namecheap renewal price $18.48, and the old Spaceship prices $8.88/$9.98 are all outdated; the prices in the table above, verified 2026-11-13, apply.

How to choose

Renewal prices: Spaceship renewal $10.68, Porkbun renewal $11.81, Namecheap renewal $19.98. Whether the first-year and renewal prices are the same: Porkbun charges the same price for all three. Whether the ICANN fee is included: included in the price at Porkbun. Privacy protection: free for life for eligible domains at Namecheap, free (for eligible domains) at Spaceship, not stated on the official pages read at Porkbun.
