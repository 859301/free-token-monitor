Cloudflare R2 vs Tigris

Updated 2026-11-14 · R2 verified 2026-11-13, Tigris verified 2026-11-14

Short answer: both have a monthly free tier and free egress. R2 has the larger free allowance (10 GB-month, 1M/10M operations per month); Tigris has a smaller one (5 GB, 10,000/100,000 requests) and states no card to start. Storage prices: R2 Standard $0.015/GB-month, Tigris Standard $0.02/GB/month. Storage classes: two on R2, four on Tigris.

Free tier

R2 free tier, metered monthly: 10 GB-month of storage, 1M Class A operations, 10M Class B operations, egress free; the free tier applies to the Standard storage class only. Tigris free tier, reset monthly: 5 GB of Standard storage, 10,000 PUT, COPY, POST and LIST requests, 100,000 GET, SELECT and other requests, egress free, no card to start; the free allowance applies to Standard storage only.

Egress rule

Egress is free on both. R2 charges no egress bandwidth fees for any storage class. The Tigris pricing table Egress row reads Free for all four storage classes. See Free-tier egress rules compared.

Storage classes

R2 has two classes: Standard and Infrequent Access ($0.01/GB-month, retrieval $0.01/GB, 30-day minimum storage, free tier excluded). Tigris has four classes: Standard $0.02/GB/month; Infrequent Access $0.01/GB/month, retrieval $0.01/GB, 30-day minimum storage; Archive $0.004/GB/month, retrieval free, 90-day minimum storage, data requires restoration before access; Archive Instant Retrieval $0.004/GB/month, retrieval $0.03/GB, 90-day minimum storage.

Operation prices

R2: Class A $4.50/M requests, Class B $0.36/M requests; which operations each class includes is unstated on the official pages read. Tigris: Class A $0.005/1,000 requests, Class B $0.0005/1,000 requests, DELETE and CANCEL free; the pricing page FAQ lists the operations in each request class — see the Tigris detail page.

How to choose

Compare the free allowance: R2 10 GB-month and 1M/10M operations per month, Tigris 5 GB and 10,000/100,000 requests. Compare the card requirement: Tigris states no card to start; the R2 card requirement is unstated on the official pages read. Compare storage classes: Tigris has four classes with minimum storage periods. Egress is free on both.

FAQ

Which has the larger free tier, R2 or Tigris?

R2: 10 GB-month of storage, 1M Class A operations, and 10M Class B operations each month; Tigris: 5 GB of Standard storage, 10,000 PUT/COPY/POST/LIST requests, and 100,000 GET/SELECT and other requests each month. Egress is free on both. R2 verified 2026-11-13, Tigris verified 2026-11-14.

Is egress free on both?

Yes. R2 charges no egress bandwidth fees for any storage class; the Tigris pricing table Egress row reads Free for all four storage classes. R2 verified 2026-11-13, Tigris verified 2026-11-14.

Which needs no card to start?

Tigris: the official pricing page states “No card to start”. The card requirement for R2 is unstated on the official pages read — check the official page. Tigris verified 2026-11-14.
