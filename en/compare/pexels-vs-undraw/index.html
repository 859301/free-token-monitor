Pexels vs unDraw: photo license vs illustration license prohibitions

Published 2026-11-15 · Updated 2026-11-15 · Verified 2026-11-14

Both allow commercial use without attribution and allow modification; all differences sit in prohibitions: Pexels expressly bans standalone sale and trademark use, while unDraw expressly bans package redistribution, competing services, and AI training. A ban stated on only one side must not be read onto the other.

Standalone definition

Only the Pexels terms define standalone: no creative effort applied and essentially the same form as on the platform; adding only a filter, color change, resize, or crop still counts as standalone; combining the content with other assets into a new creative work does not. The unDraw license page has no standalone wording; it bans package redistribution and compiling competing services.

AI training ban

Only the unDraw license page expressly bans AI training: training, fine-tuning, or developing AI, ML models, or similar technology, including use as generative AI training data, inclusion in ML datasets, fine-tuning models, training/validating/testing AI systems, and automated processing. The Pexels terms portions read do not address AI training — check the official page.

Trademark-use ban

Only the Pexels terms expressly ban use of content as a trademark, design mark, trade name, business name, or service mark, or as part of one. The unDraw license page states no general trademark-use ban — check the official page.

Brand rules

Both have brand-related rules pointing in different directions: Pexels bars content depicting trademarks from commercial use for goods and services; unDraw provides brand logos in illustrations only to present the brand itself. Neither rule equals a trademark license.

FAQ

Can both be used commercially?

Yes, both without attribution and with modification allowed. Differences sit in prohibitions: standalone sale, package redistribution, competing services, AI training, and trademark use are distributed differently across the two — see the table.

Can unDraw illustrations train an AI model?

No. The unDraw license page expressly prohibits it; such use needs separate express written permission from unDraw. The Pexels terms portions read do not address AI training — check the official page.

Can a Pexels photo be printed on merchandise for sale?

Standalone printing on products for sale is prohibited; content depicting trademarks must especially not be printed on products for sale or other physical products. Combining the content with other assets into a new creative work does not count as standalone.
