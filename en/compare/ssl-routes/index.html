Two free SSL routes

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: the Cloudflare Free plan at $0 includes Universal SSL, along with DNS, CDN, Unmetered DDoS Protection, and WAF; Let's Encrypt issues free DV certificates, requested via ACME and subject to rate limits (50 certificates/registered domain/7 days, 5 certificates with the same identifier set/7 days, 300 orders/account/3 hours, up to 100 identifiers per certificate), with ARI renewals exempt from all rate limits. How to choose

DNS and CDN already on Cloudflare: the Free plan includes Universal SSL. Certificates installed on a server: Let's Encrypt free DV certificates, requested via the ACME process, subject to rate limits, with ARI renewals exempt from all rate limits.
