Usage units differ

Supabase counts storage (500 MB), egress (5 GB), and API requests (unlimited); Neon counts compute (100 CU-hrs/project/month) and storage (1 GB/project); Turso counts row reads/writes (500M row reads / 10M row writes per month) and syncs (3 GB/month). The units differ, so the numbers cannot be compared directly by size; each figure applies to its own unit.

Pause and overage

Supabase: pause after 1 week inactive, max 2 active projects. Neon: scale to zero after 5 minutes idle, overage keeps data with compute paused until the next cycle. Turso: the FAQ states a Free plan overage requires upgrade or blocking; idle-pause rules are not stated on the official pages read.

Cards

Neon and Turso state no credit card on the official site. Supabase card requirements are not stated explicitly in the captured official pricing page text — check the official page.

Acquisition

The Supabase official blog announced the acquisition of Turso on 2026-10-02, stating existing users are unaffected and Turso keeps operating, with no pricing or free-tier policy change announced.

How to choose

For stated permanent wording with no card: Neon. For database, auth, storage, and functions in one free tier: Supabase (500 MB database, 50,000 MAU, 1 GB file storage, 500,000 Edge Function invocations), with a 1-week-inactivity pause and a 2-project limit. For 100 databases with row read/write units: Turso, with overage requiring upgrade or blocking.
