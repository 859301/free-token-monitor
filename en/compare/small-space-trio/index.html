Free subdomains: yes or no

GoogieHost: 1 website + free subdomain (suffixes written three ways: whf.bz/cu.ma, hf.bz, googiehost.com). FreeHosting.com: a self-controlled registered domain is required; free subdomains are not hosted; free domains such as .TK/.ML/.GA are not accepted. FreeHostia: the plan page shows 5 hosted domains and 15 subdomains (TOS); a free subdomain is not shown on the plan page.

Is SSL free

GoogieHost: a free SSL certificate activates automatically, with HTTPS available from day one. FreeHosting.com: the free tier includes no free SSL; HTTPS + a free SSL certificate is a $29.95 one-time add-on. FreeHostia: the Security section shows a Let’s Encrypt SSL certificate.

Database size

GoogieHost: counts disagree across two pages (the plan table states 1 database vs the PHP page’s “multiple databases”). FreeHosting.com: 1 MySQL database, InnoDB, phpMyAdmin, no query count/query time limits; +1 database costs a $24.95 one-time add-on. FreeHostia: 1 MySQL v.5 database, 10 MB storage; hourly query and concurrency limits sit in the TOS CPU terms.

FAQ

Without a domain, which of the three hosts fits?

GoogieHost has a free subdomain. FreeHosting.com requires an owned domain. A FreeHostia free subdomain is not shown on the plan page — check the official page. Verified 2026-11-13.

Which of the three hosts has free SSL?

GoogieHost activates free SSL automatically; FreeHostia shows a Let’s Encrypt SSL certificate. FreeHosting.com SSL is a $29.95 one-time add-on. Verified 2026-11-13.
