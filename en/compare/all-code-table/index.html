Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: 2 entries at a glance: GitHub Free and GitLab Free, both $0 free tiers. GitHub places no limit on repository counts, with 2,000 CI/CD minutes per month; GitLab allows 5 users per top-level group, with 400 compute minutes per month, and sign-up needs no credit card. Neither pricing page states permanent wording on the official pages read.

FAQ

Is the free tier permanent?

Neither pricing page states permanent wording on the official pages read — check the official page. GitHub Free is $0; GitLab Free is $0 with no credit card required at sign-up. Verified 2026-11-13.

Does sign-up need a card?

The GitLab Free pricing page states no credit card is required, but shared runners require card verification ($1 authorization check, no charge). GitHub Free card requirements are not stated on the official pages read. Verified 2026-11-13.
