All code hosting compared

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14)

Short answer: 5 entries at a glance, all $0 free tiers: GitHub Free places no limit on repository counts, with 2,000 CI/CD minutes per month; GitLab Free allows 5 users per top-level group, with 400 compute minutes per month, and sign-up needs no credit card; Bitbucket Free allows up to 5 users, with 50 build minutes per month; Codeberg free hosting serves free/libre software projects only, with 100 repositories by default; Azure DevOps free allowances cover the first 5 users, with 1,800 pipeline minutes per month. No pricing page states permanent wording on the official pages read.

FAQ

Is the free tier permanent?

No pricing page states permanent wording on the official pages read — check the official page. GitHub Free is $0; GitLab Free is $0 with no credit card required at sign-up; Bitbucket Free is $0 for up to 5 users; Codeberg requires no payment for any service; Azure DevOps offers the first 5 users free. Verified 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14.

Does sign-up need a card?

The GitLab Free pricing page states no credit card is required, but shared runners require card verification ($1 authorization check, no charge). The Azure DevOps pricing page FAQ states no credit card is necessary to get started. The Codeberg FAQ states no payment is required for any service. GitHub Free and Bitbucket Free card requirements are not stated on the official pages read. Verified 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14.
