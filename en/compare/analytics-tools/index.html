Metering differs

Umami counts events: Hobby 100,000 events, 3 websites. PostHog counts per-product allowances: Analytics 1M events/month, Session replay 5K recordings/month, Feature flags 1M requests/month, Error tracking 100K exceptions/month, Surveys 1500 responses/month, Logs 10 GB/month, AI Observability 100K events/month. Plausible tiers by website count: Starter 1, Growth up to 3, Business up to 10 websites. GA4 limits retention periods and query quotas. Clarity has no traffic limits.

Retention and sampling

Retention: Umami 6 months, PostHog 1 year, GA4 user-level 2 or 14 months, event-level 2/14/26/38/50 months (26/38/50 for 360 only); Plausible and Clarity retention periods are not stated on the official pages read. Sampling: Clarity has no sampling; GA4 event-level queries: Standard 10 million events, 360 up to 1 billion events; the other three are not stated on the official pages read.

Overage behavior

PostHog usage stops at the limit; Umami paid plans bill overage per event, Hobby overage behavior not stated; Plausible has no surprise charges, with an upgrade notice after two consecutive months over tier; GA4 overage behavior is not stated on the official pages read; Clarity has no traffic limits.

Self-hosting

Umami is open source, MIT license, free to self-host. PostHog is open source, MIT licensed. Plausible Community Edition is free to self-host, AGPLv3, tracking script MIT. GA4 and Clarity self-hosting is not stated on the official pages read.

How to choose

Four points: free tier or trial (Plausible has only a trial), metering (events, per-product allowances, retention and quotas, no traffic limits), retention and sampling (6 months, 1 year, tiered retention, no sampling), overage behavior (stops at the limit, billed per event, upgrade notice, not stated). For self-hosting, see Umami, PostHog, and Plausible.
