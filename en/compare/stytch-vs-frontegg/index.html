Free user counts and units

Stytch covers 10,000 monthly active users and AI agents free; the comparison table unit is “External active members and agents”, with counting details unstated on the pages read. Frontegg covers 7,500 MAU; MAU means a user who logs in at least once in a calendar month, billed on users who actually log in. The units are worded differently, so the numbers cannot be compared directly by size.

Connections and organizations

Both include 5 SSO/SCIM connections and unlimited organizations. On counting, Frontegg states SSO and SCIM are counted separately, with a customer using both counting as two connections; Stytch connection counting details are not stated on the pages read — check the official page.

Custom domains and cards

Frontegg includes a custom domain, and the FAQ states no credit card is needed to start. Stytch custom domains and card requirements are not stated on the pages read — check the official page.

Overage rules

Stytch states $125 per connection beyond 5; the per-user overage price is unstated. Frontegg overage prices and behavior are not stated on the pages read — check the official page.

How to choose

By user count: Stytch 10,000, Frontegg 7,500, with differently worded units. By custom domain and card: Frontegg includes a custom domain and needs no card to start; Stytch follows the official page on both. By M2M: Stytch includes 1,000 M2M tokens; the Frontegg free M2M allowance follows the official page. By overage: Stytch states a $125 per-connection overage price; Frontegg overage prices follow the official page.
