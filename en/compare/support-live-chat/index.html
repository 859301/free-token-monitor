Seats and metering

Tawk.to seats are unlimited, while Crisp and Chatwoot Cloud allow 2 seats each. The Crisp 100 customer profile cap is a separate limit, counted apart from conversation metering; Tawk.to and Chatwoot Cloud conversation caps — check the official page.

Branding

Tawk.to branding removal carries a stated price ($39/month or $348/year); Crisp white labelling sits in the Plus tier ($295/month); Chatwoot Cloud free tier branding — check the official page.

Channels and feature placement

Tawk.to ticketing and knowledge base appear in the free core capabilities; Crisp knowledge base sits in Essentials and ticketing in Plus; Chatwoot Cloud help center coverage — check the official page. On channels, the Crisp Free tier covers the website chat widget and contact form, with the omnichannel inbox in Essentials.

How to choose

With many agents and paid add-ons acceptable, Tawk.to fits; with 2 agents and 100 customer profiles or fewer, Crisp fits; with an existing Chatwoot setup or an open source preference, Chatwoot Cloud fits, with the self-hosted edition as the next comparison.
