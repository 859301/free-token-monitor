Merchant of Record fees compared

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: four MoR fee structures: Paddle and Lemon Squeezy charge a single fee of 5% + 50¢ per transaction with no monthly fee; Polar uses four tiers, with higher monthly fees and lower per-transaction rates, from Starter 5% + 50¢ to Scale 3.4% + 30¢; Stripe Managed Payments charges 3.5% + Payments fees, added together. On surcharges, Polar states international cards +1.5% and disputes $15 each; Lemon Squeezy states transactions outside the US may carry a small additional fee.

Four fee structures

Paddle and Lemon Squeezy charge a single fee with no monthly fee, so zero sales means zero fees. Polar charges by monthly-fee tier: the higher the monthly fee, the lower the per-transaction fee, with official break-even points per tier. Stripe Managed Payments charges 3.5% plus Payments fees, with the Payments-fee part calculated at standard processing rates (US domestic cards 2.9% + 30¢, international cards +1.5%).

How the fixed part affects small transactions

The fixed parts — 50¢, 40¢, 35¢, 30¢ — do not change with the transaction amount: the smaller the transaction, the larger the fixed part’s share. Calculated from the official rates, a $5 transaction at 5% + 50¢ costs $0.75, or 15%; a $100 transaction at the same rate costs $5.50, or 5.5%.

Surcharges compared

Only Polar states surcharge figures: international cards +1.5%, disputes $15 each, payout fees passed through at Stripe cost. Lemon Squeezy states transactions outside the US may carry a small additional fee, with details on the docs page. Paddle and Stripe Managed Payments surcharge details: check the official page.

FAQ

Which MoR has the lowest fee?

By official rates, Polar Scale at 3.4% + 30¢ ($400/month) is lower than Paddle and Lemon Squeezy at 5% + 50¢; Stripe Managed Payments is 3.5% + Payments fees. Monthly fees and break-even points are on the Polar detail page. Verified 2026-11-13.

Do MoR fees include tax services?

Yes. Paddle collects the correct tax rate, tracks filing deadlines, and submits sales tax filings; Lemon Squeezy collects, remits, and files sales tax/VAT; Stripe Managed Payments includes indirect tax compliance in 75+ countries. Verified 2026-11-13.
