FAQ

Which of the 4 has the lowest fee?

Stripe standard processing at 2.9% + 30¢ (US domestic cards) is lower than the three MoR starting fees of 5% + 50¢; Polar Scale is 3.4% + 30¢ ($400/month). Beyond the fee numbers, the differences are in tax and compliance responsibility and supported countries. Verified 2026-11-13.

Which of the 4 needs no card at signup?

Only Lemon Squeezy states no card required on the pricing page. The other 3 do not state it on the official pages read — check the official page. Verified 2026-11-13.
