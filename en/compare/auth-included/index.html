Standalone vs database-included auth

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: standalone auth services handle auth only: Clerk Hobby at 50,000 MRUs per app and Auth0 Free at up to 25,000 MAU. Database-included auth comes with database free tiers: Supabase Auth at 50,000 MAU (with the Supabase free tier) and Firebase Auth at 50K MAU (with the Firebase free allowances). Full specs for the included allowances are on the database detail pages. The units differ: Clerk counts MRU, the other three count MAU.

Different homes

Clerk and Auth0 are standalone auth services; the free tiers cover auth only. Supabase Auth and Firebase Auth are part of database free tiers: Supabase Auth comes with the Supabase free tier, Firebase Auth comes with the Firebase free allowances. Card, overage, and pause rules for the included allowances sit in the same detail pages as the database free tiers; see the database detail pages.

Units of measurement

Clerk counts MRU: a user counts as retained when returning 24 hours after signing up. Auth0, Supabase, and Firebase count MAU; Auth0 words it “External Active Users”. MAU counting details are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. The units differ, so the numbers cannot be compared directly by size.

How to choose

With Supabase or Firebase already in use as the database, the included auth allowances come with the database free tiers; the numbers are in the table above, full specs on the database detail pages. For auth only, without a database, the standalone auth free tiers apply: Clerk vs Auth0 compares Clerk Hobby and Auth0 Free item by item.

No. Clerk counts MRU (retained users), the other three count MAU; the units differ. MAU counting details are not stated on the official pages read — check the official page. Verified 2026-11-13.
