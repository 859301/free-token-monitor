Cost structure

Self-hosting shifts costs to server spend and maintenance time, with pricing itself on the official page. Hosted billing follows tier rules, with paid tiers from $19/agent/month and a 15-day free trial.

Requirements

Self-hosting needs a Linux server, PostgreSQL, and Redis, with S3 or S3-compatible storage for file uploads, deployment guides for Docker, Kubernetes, and major cloud providers, and at least 4GB of memory and 2 CPU cores recommended for production.

Support and AI

Community Edition support channels are Discord and GitHub; Premium Support adds priority email support, and Enterprise adds phone support and developer office hours. Captain AI comes with the Premium Support and Enterprise tiers on the self-hosted side, connecting to OpenAI GPT models; on the cloud side, paid tier allowances run 300/500/800 a month, with extra allowances at $20 per 1000.

How to choose

With data ownership valued and a server plus maintenance time available, the self-hosted edition fits; with hands-off hosting preferred and 2 agents enough, the Cloud free tier fits. Migration between the two keeps data and settings.
