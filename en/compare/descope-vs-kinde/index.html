User counts, tenants, and organizations

Descope covers 7,500 MAU and 10 monthly active tenants; an active tenant has two or more associated users with at least one logging in within a month. Kinde covers 10,500 MAU and 5 monthly active organizations; organizations with a single active user are not counted toward MAO, and users on paid subscriptions are not counted toward MAU. Tenant and organization units differ, so 10 and 5 cannot be compared directly by size.

SSO connections

Descope includes 3 SSO connections on the free tier; one tenant can have more than one SSO connection, and every configured connection counts. Kinde includes 1 enterprise SSO connection on the free tier; enterprise SSO is unlimited with no additional costs from Plus up.

Overage rules

Descope has no overages: exceeding any limit requires upgrading to Pro, with a warning email and a grace period to upgrade after exceeding the MAU or tenant limit; the grace period length is unstated. Kinde bills overages past included limits automatically at published rates; Free-tier overage rates do not appear in the table read.

Custom domains and cards

Kinde includes a custom domain and needs no credit card. Descope has no custom domain on the free tier; custom domains start on Pro; Descope card requirements are not stated on the pages read — check the official page.

How to choose

By user count: Descope 7,500 MAU, Kinde 10,500 MAU. By tenants and organizations: Descope 10 monthly active tenants, Kinde 5 monthly active organizations, with different units. By SSO: Descope 3, Kinde 1. By overage: Descope hard limits requiring an upgrade, Kinde automatic billing. By custom domain: Kinde includes one, Descope starts custom domains on Pro.

FAQ

How do Descope and Kinde differ past the free limits?

Descope has no overages: exceeding any limit requires upgrading to Pro, with a warning email and a grace period to upgrade after exceeding the MAU or tenant limit (length unstated). Kinde bills overages past included limits automatically at published rates; Free-tier overage rates do not appear in the table read. Verified 2026-11-14.

Which free tier includes a custom domain?

Kinde includes a custom domain; Descope has no custom domain on the free tier, with custom domains starting on Pro (from $249/month, billed annually). Verified 2026-11-14.
