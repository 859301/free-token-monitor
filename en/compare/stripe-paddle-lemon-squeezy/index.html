Supported countries are the first difference

The Stripe official supported country/region list has 50 entries; mainland China is not on the list, and Hong Kong is on the list. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy supported countries and verification requirements are not stated on the official pages read; signup eligibility follows each platform’s official supported-country/verification pages.

Fee structures differ

Stripe standard processing costs less than the two MoR services (2.9% + 30¢, US domestic cards), but tax and compliance responsibility stays with the seller; Managed Payments adds MoR services at 3.5% + Payments fees. The Paddle and Lemon Squeezy 5% + 50¢ rates include MoR services.

MoR versus payment processing

A Merchant of Record acts as the seller of record in a transaction, taking on tax, fraud protection, refund, and related responsibilities; payment processing only processes payments. Paddle and Lemon Squeezy are themselves Merchant of Record services; Stripe provides MoR services through the Managed Payments product.
