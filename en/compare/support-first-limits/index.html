Seats hit first

Crisp, Chatwoot Cloud, HubSpot, and Freshdesk free seats number 2, so a third person hits the cap; Zoho Desk and Jira Service Management allow 3; Help Scout allows up to 5; Tawk.to seats are unlimited.

Contacts and profiles hit first

Help Scout counts 100 contacts a month by person, with multiple conversations from one person counting as one contact; Crisp caps customer profiles at 100. When inquiry volume grows by person count, these caps hit before seat caps.

Branding and features hit first

On branding, Tawk.to removal carries a stated price, Crisp white labelling sits in Plus, and HubSpot removal starts in Starter. On features, Crisp knowledge base and ticketing, Zoho Desk instant messaging and knowledge base, and HubSpot knowledge base and customer portal all sit in higher tiers; needing those features hits before usage caps.

Duration hits first

Freshdesk runs 6 months free, so duration itself is a cap; the Crisp 14-day trial and Zoho Desk 15-day trial are separate matters, with post-trial handling on the official page.
