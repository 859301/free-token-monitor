Monitor counts and intervals

UptimeRobot: 50 monitors, 5-minute interval. Sentry: 1 uptime monitor, check interval not stated on the official pages read. Sentry also has 1 cron monitor.

Status page

UptimeRobot Free includes a basic status page, 1 status page. Sentry status pages are not stated on the official pages read. On the self-hosted route, UptimeFlare includes a status page per forum post title wording.

Self-hosted route

Uptime Kuma, UptimeFlare, and Nezha Monitoring are open-source self-hosted options; a runtime environment is needed, and parameters follow each project’s page.

How to choose

For multiple monitors with a stated check interval, see UptimeRobot’s 50 monitors and 5-minute interval. For Sentry error tracking with only 1 uptime monitor needed, see Sentry Developer’s 1 uptime monitor. For control over the runtime environment, see the self-hosted route.
