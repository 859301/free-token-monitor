Minimum interval comparison

cron-job.org allows every-minute execution; QStash schedule resolution reaches 1 minute; EasyCron Free has a 20-minute minimum interval, with no allowance for every-minute or every-10-minute jobs; the Inngest cron minimum interval is not stated on the pages read.

Timeout and retry comparison

EasyCron Free times out at 5 seconds; QStash allows a 15-minute maximum HTTP response duration; Inngest allows a 2-hour maximum step duration, without extending a shorter host timeout; cron-job.org timeouts are per job in seconds, with the default value unstated. On retries, only QStash states a default of 3 retries with a backoff table on the pages read; the other three services leave retry rules or counts unstated on the pages read.

Timezone comparison

cron-job.org allows a timezone per job, UTC by default; QStash supports timezones; Inngest cron uses UTC by default, with a TZ= prefix, and may run zero, one, or two times on daylight saving change days, with no daylight saving correction; EasyCron timezone support is not stated on the pages read.

Choosing

For URL-only scheduled triggers under no-warranty terms, cron-job.org carries the widest free scope; for low-frequency jobs (hourly or daily) under monthly expiry, EasyCron job-count allowances are wider; for schedules needing retries, delays, and a dead-letter queue, QStash and Inngest queue and workflow capabilities apply.

FAQ

Which service fits an every-minute schedule?

cron-job.org allows every-minute execution, and QStash schedule resolution reaches 1 minute; EasyCron Free has a 20-minute minimum interval, with no every-minute allowance.

Which service needs no card?

The cron-job.org Terms of Service state no fees have to be paid; the Inngest pricing page states no credit card is required. QStash and EasyCron card requirements are not stated on the pages read — check the official page.
