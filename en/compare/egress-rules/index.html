B2: the 3x rule

Reading egress costs

Compare three points: whether egress is charged, what the free part is measured by, and the per-unit price beyond it. R2 egress is free of charge; B2 direct egress measures the free part as 3x average monthly stored data, $0.01/GB beyond that; B2 egress via partner CDNs is unlimited free.

FAQ

What does egress mean?

Egress is the traffic produced when data moves out of a storage service. Egress rules: R2 charges nothing, B2 bills under the 3x rule or the partner CDN rule. Verified 2026-11-13.

Does storing more raise the free egress amount on B2?

The free egress amount on B2 is measured as 3x average monthly stored data. How average stored data is calculated — check the official page. Verified 2026-11-13.
