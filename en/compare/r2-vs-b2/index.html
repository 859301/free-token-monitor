Cloudflare R2 vs Backblaze B2

Updated 2026-11-14 · Table data from vendor official pages (verified 2026-11-13)

Short answer: R2 has a monthly-metered free tier, with egress free of charge; B2 is pay-as-you-go, with no free tier, and egress billed under the 3x rule. Storage prices: R2 standard storage $0.015/GB-month, B2 $6.95/TB/30 days. Egress rules are the main difference between the two; see the table below.

Free tier vs pay-as-you-go

R2 free tier, metered monthly: each month 10 GB-month storage, 1M Class A operations, 10M Class B operations, egress free. B2 has no free tier: the official pricing page showed no “10GB free” wording in either of two captures; storage and egress are billed pay-as-you-go.

Egress rules

R2 charges no egress bandwidth fees for any storage class. B2 direct egress is free up to 3x average monthly stored data, $0.01/GB beyond that; egress via partner CDNs (Fastly, Cloudflare, bunny.net, CacheFly, CoreWeave, Equinix Metal, Vultr, phoenixNAP) is unlimited free. Full comparison: Egress rules compared.

Storage classes and Overdrive

R2 has two storage classes, Standard and Infrequent Access: Infrequent Access costs $0.01/GB-month, retrieval $0.01/GB, 30-day minimum storage, and the free tier does not apply. B2 Overdrive costs $15/TB/month; terms beyond the price — check the official page.

How to choose

For a monthly free allowance with egress free of charge, compare the R2 free allowance and prices. For pay-as-you-go with partner-CDN egress rules, compare the B2 prices and egress rules. Database-bundled storage: see Standalone vs database-bundled storage.

FAQ

Which has the larger free allowance, R2 or B2?

B2 has no free tier, so no comparison by free allowance is possible. R2 monthly free allowance: 10 GB-month storage, 1M Class A operations, 10M Class B operations, egress free. Verified 2026-11-13.

How does egress charging differ between the two?

R2 charges no egress for any storage class. B2 direct egress is free up to 3x average monthly stored data, $0.01/GB beyond that, and egress via partner CDNs is unlimited free. Verified 2026-11-13.

Is the B2 10GB free claim true?

The official pricing page showed no “10GB free” wording in either of two captures. B2 is pay-as-you-go, with no free tier. Verified 2026-11-13.
