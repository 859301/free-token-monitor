Free-tier egress rules compared

Updated 2026-11-14 · R2 and B2 verified 2026-11-13; Tigris, Google Cloud Storage, and UploadThing verified 2026-11-14

Short answer: R2 and Tigris egress free for all storage classes. Google Cloud Storage: 100 GB of outbound transfer from North America free each month (excluding China and Australia destinations), general data transfer from $0.08/GiB beyond that. UploadThing egress charging is unstated on the official pages read. B2 has no free tier and bills direct egress under the 3x rule, with unlimited free egress via partner CDNs.

Free egress on both

R2: the official pricing page states that no bandwidth charges apply to egress for any storage class. Tigris: the official pricing page states that other clouds charge for every gigabyte that leaves and Tigris does not; the Egress row of the pricing table reads Free for all four storage classes.

Free egress allowance

Google Cloud Storage: 100 GB of outbound data transfer from North America to all region destinations free each month, excluding China and Australia destinations; the Free Tier applies only to usage in the us-east1, us-west1, and us-central1 regions. Beyond the allowance, general data transfer starts at $0.08/GiB.

Egress charging unstated

UploadThing: the official pages read do not state upload/download charging — check the official page.

Pay-as-you-go reference

B2 has no free tier and bills by usage. Direct egress is free up to 3x average monthly stored data, $0.01/GB beyond that; egress via 8 partner CDNs is unlimited and free. See Egress rules compared.

Which services have completely free egress?

R2 and Tigris: egress is free for all storage classes. R2 verified 2026-11-13, Tigris verified 2026-11-14.

Beyond the allowance, general data transfer starts at $0.08/GiB. Verified 2026-11-14.
